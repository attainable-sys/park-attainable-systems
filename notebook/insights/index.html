1000.009 · 1000 living and loving · journal · 2024.08.31

And here we are, back to the same methods that we were using to keep ourselves on track - the same kind of logic that brought us to the same kinds of conclusions.

She pauses so that she can check the weather which, unrelenting, is rain that doesn't seem to want to stop, and means that she's going to have to go and run on the treadmill

Trying to align myself with the approaches that mean I'm going to actually stick to my ideals, the ways that I want to write and the ways that I want to force myself to write. The two-and-a-half pages that I want to force myself to write every day on paper but since there's no feedback mechanism, there really isn't a way for me to force myself into it. Constraint, the one thing that I don't seem capable of doing for myself.
The other part that stands out or that I make myself aware of is the face that in the entirety of my time working on the matter, there's not really anything that is making me pay the abundance of attention that I'd like to force myself into as a result of all of this work. I'm telling myself that there's a goal in mind, and I'm telling myself that I have to do what it necessary in order to force myself into the outcome, but I don't think I have ever known what discipline is, at least not in the context of what I'm actually capable of what what it is that I've sought out.

The insight that I've had from years of writing at this point is that most of it is in seeking catharsis, and that the 'warmup' of having had to think about what goes into the writing is what stands the most in my way - the realization that time is something that Id on't ahve a lot of control over until I do (because I do, and I realize that I do, and I forget that I do more often than I don't). Remembering that time is a function of novelty, and that what is most powerful about writing is that it is the constant generation of novelty. It's the ability to fabricate and connect the facts so that the information fits together in a way that creates outcomes more aligned with the spirit rather than the real - what is the spiritual versus the nihilist? What is the goal of thinking in the ways that we do, and what is it that creates this outcome?

the rain gets harder, as though to confirm that she shouldn't have been out there on the road, pounding pavement while being soaked to the bone, just having recovered from a 24-hour cold

And I think there's something to this, the idea that novelty is something that can be aspired to and broken apart, that there's a way to short-cut the human desire to get to what we expect of ourselves and the means through which we do so.

the lake is nearly invisible behind the sheet of rain between her and its distance. yes, she could have been out there, but it would have extended the illness

The realization is then that there's some need for creativity that functions in control of time, in service of the need to create discipline and aspire to more of who we want to be rather than what it is that we default to. I find that this is the most important part ot explore, hearing from oneself that there's something that we haven't encountered and that there is a torrent of voices that can come forth when you choose to slow down and pay attention to them, and that in these voices there is a collection of thoughts that underly one's actions and can be directed to, can be attended to and cared for, because they have been there and because they deserve to be there in the ways that they show up.

I've never been one to have discipline because I don't default to it, but I definitely aspire to it. If routine is that which speeds up time, there's a way to hack that routine into the exploration of the mind that slows down specific parts of the routine so that there is some liberty in the way that routine is adhered to - parts of what the routine can perform and what it is that it can do ahead of becoming something better than a repetitive function that creates sameness. That's not what writing does, that's not its service function, and it's not how I will ever pursue it.

However, how do I find myself tied to this goal? What's the real function of it? Is it to lock in and create a way for me to find bigger and better parts of myself? Do I want to explore bigger and better worlds that I've created? Do I want to increase my wpm just so that there's an easier transference of thought into the page? It's unclear.

the rain continues, the lake is invisible, she's destined to run on the treadmill today
