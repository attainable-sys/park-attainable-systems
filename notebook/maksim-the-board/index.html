Maksim - the board

1010.001 · 1010 faith and coordination · fiction · 2023.04.18

I added colors to it, pictures of places that I wanted to be, and people that I wanted in my life. I couldn't add flavors, but I added pictures of the foods that brought together those flavors, and I couldn't add touch, so I added pictures of the things that I wanted to touch, lower backs, hot sauce, sandstone, rain evaporating from warm surfaces. I added pressure that causes blooming, though I couldn't add this as a picture, so I just added cicadas. I added peace and books because those are things that matter to me, and I added ideas, but I couldn't figure out a picture for it so I added thought bubbles and speech bubbles that came from them. I added the feeling of running forever, but it was just a picture of running shoes.

I added what I thought was missing from my life, over and over, thinking that enough of me filling the space with these feelings might mitigate the loneliness that I felt, as though somehow I'd fill the gap of my own intentions by dreaming of something that I didn't have and as though thinking in this way was the correct aopproach to trying to build out hte future that you want.

You have to believe it. You have to believe that what is possible is probable, and you have to move progressively toward it. Quiet times are times of action, times in which the effort that can be made outside of the pressures of everyday life are something that can put you on your back in terms of the efforts, that can be made rather than the frustration that girds inaction. I think mostly of hte way that ttime is not osmetihng that we think of as a resource, but rather something that is simply passing.

Maksim was the person who told me this, that I needed to stop thinking of time as something that was simply passing, and start thinking about it as something that could be spent wisely - that if your time is being spent cheaply, then you are not valuing your time for the resource that it is. Ho told me that I hadn't been thinking about it enough and the passage of time was something that I needed to pay more attention to, a man in my heart who was both old and young, both wise and headstrong, but a maker, the sort of person who can solve any issues provided they have the tools, and if Maksim didn't have the tools, he would make them out of the other tools that he did have.

When I got back in touch with him, he told me a number of things that I had forgotten- that my life is full of limitless potential and that the ways through which I engage in that potential have much to do with my ability to force myself into the future. He also told me that I was not using my time wisely, and he was surprised at how much time had passed since we last worked together. I asked him when we last saw one another and he told me that we worked together a bit when I was 20, but the first time we met was 14. What had I been doing since then? I'm not sure I said, but I think I didn't want to admit that so much of my time had been eaten up by thinking that one pursuit was going to give me meaning and that I got lost in the weeds of realizing that it would never be fulfilling to pursue what others were pursuing.

I think that at some level he knew this, and I could see that he was frustrated with the time that had passed, I can't blame him for it - if he really thinks the last time we worked together was over a decade ago, it's probably worth the rust to think that moving quickly is going to be more difficult. He didn't seem as though he was hopeless, though - he seemed engaged, wanting to build something more.

That's when he told me about all the things that he'd had to make without knowing their purpose, all the tools that I didn't realize I had available but that he'd handed to whomever was handing them to me. That was you? I asked a number of times, and he would just nod sagely for a moment. I didn't know what I was making it for but I'm glad you put it to use, and I'm glad it was for you and then he'd twirl the ball-peen hammer that always seemed as though it was in his hands. I can make anything you need me to make.

When I asked him what he'd rather be doing, he mentioned that there is something really cool that we can make together, I just don't think you've ever put the time into thinking about how we'd make it and I was not sure what he was referring to. He showed me blueprints for a a shape something like a building, a church, a gathering space, where the inside was filled with interleaving surfaces letting the light in from outside, where the power of it was wonder for those who would enter, where things could be said and mentioned for their gravity and to cement people into understanding that there was something wondrous to pay attention to, but that the only way we could get to making this was if you can offer me the time to make it.

I left that conversation thinking that Maksim was ready for me to offer him something I didn't know how to offer, because I'd spent so much of my time thinking that what I needed to make was something so much more surrounded by what is around me - and I think this is true, even now as I write this the pressure that I feel from what surrounds me has more to do with other people and other places and expectations that are set on me, not what I hold inside of myself, not what I think I should have inside, and not what I wanted to build back when I knew Maksim at first. You'll have to let me apologize I wanted to say and every time I was about to, he'd stop me and remind me that he didn't need an apology, I exist outside of Time. I can make anything, and I can make it at whatever pace I want to, but you are aging. You are thirty-two. You are the only coming to me with something that you don't have much of, and I want to remind you that while I want to make these things with you, it's you who holds the raw materials with which to do so. You can choose to offer Time to me, you could offer all of it, but I will not ask for that much - but you cannot forget that if Time is a resource and you only have so much, you need to remember how you are spending it. Don't spend it on the things that don't matter, but if it was spent on the things that do, then it was worth the cost.
