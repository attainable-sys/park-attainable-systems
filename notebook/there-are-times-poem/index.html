There are times (poem)

1050.026 · 1050 writing · poem · 2024.09.21

There are times i can feel it roaring

It is a notion to attend nor
Is the weight of it
Something that brings me to the closeness
I am earning

There are times where I have turned myself over
To the notion that
Things need not get better
there is something holy
made from knowing oneself
and confidently stripping away
the sea of worry

We have earned the keep,
the way of becoming
accompanying not having wavered
Atterizaje, a grounded tree
The same words uttered over and over,
In the feeling of my death echo
reverberating back through time toward me

Grace, I have known you
though those times
the hoary weight of time
I have feared those same things
that take harbor inside of me

Grace, I have known you
through those times
Hearing and respecting
In whatever caused me

Grace I have known you hearing me
In the best of those times
Not having thought that this was a place
That I would stop calling home

Grace
I did think that this time
this would be home
and so I stretched myself across it
as though it would be such a place
within which I would find
that safety

I bored into the landscape
With the desire to sink into it skin
And feel the moss veins of age it would offer

Now I am lucky to know something for sure
In exchange for what it gave me;
A family, estrangement
Telling me that I am loved
unwavering
known only by ocean
or by coastline

I think I heard far too late
that diminishing echo

So my reaction when it was made louder
By the canyon walls by which I was surrounded
It was not something to which I was ready to react

it enveloped me
and i heard it
And knew better then than I had before
That i am not home
And I am looking for it
and that it is not home that will find me

Grace, give me mention
for i spent the least time wandering
In places that offered me
unbridled joy
places into which I sank
Grace I know better where home is now but
I also know better that it is not a place i will find as i wander

Settled into my sorrow
I know I brought you here to let pass
the empty gravel of searching
ungraded and churning beneath my feet
The return to my roots
The emptying of bookcases and living rooms and
Meals and places
I wanted more of and
haven’t mentioned yet missing.

00
Dear the two of you,
