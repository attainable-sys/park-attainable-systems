Bread at Grand Army Plaza (poem)

1050.029 · 1050 writing · poem · 2025.01.17

Someone dumped several loaves worth of bread
Across Grand army Plaza and
Even though it was winter
I couldn't really make sense
Of why there were no pigeons

Stray
What is left of me across the same space
So that in knowing
I could have somehow left part of myself behind
For you to consume

--

I wish those were mountains
I could see just beyond the tree line
In Prospect Park
In the middle of the winter
When clouds choose to be dense enough
That we can't see through them
And we can pretend that they are landscaped instead

We can pretend that there are things far away
The cover higher ground
Over more distance
And that they're an option to climb

Buy me time with
The way you speak to me
Susurrations of that Horizon
Falling off with the curvature of the earth

Buy me time with
The way you have chosen to show yourself
On the Advent of sunset
When it is coldest
And when when my heart feels the most broken

Buy me time with
The mountains that choose to make themselves
Of the densest clouds
The carry snow for this part
Of the year

Buy me time
With sunlight that scrapes
The bottom of a cloud bank

I will not break
Against the bulkhead
Of that optimism
Nor will I allow myself
To be eaten alive by
A Sun never rising again

And I will be shaking leaves in the wind,
Remembering the park benches I could have slept on
Where the sun sets I'd failed to capture

Buy me time with the vastness of your love
With countenance is a form of lullaby
Of course I haven't swallowed the Sun, My son

--

How do I prevent you
From feeling as though
You have become invisible

How do I fill your days with
Pride and
The poetry of other people's smiles

How do I find the space
To acknowledge that you
Have made yourself bigger
All the while
Finding yourself
More comfortable in the frame
That you have been offered

Your invisibility
Rehoboam
I have taken you home in my teeth
And so you'll dissolve with time

-
