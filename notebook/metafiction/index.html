Metafiction

1050.033 · 1050 writing · craft

Metafiction is a form of writing wherein the writer often breaks the fourth wall by playing with the structure of narrative to pull the reader into the act of reading. It’s something that is often seen in the writing of Kundera, M, but as well in Cortazar, J and Labatut, B.

With Kundera, M in particular, the metafictional elements are present in his divergences into philosophical footnotes and/or commentary on the story itself, particular with the effort to have the reader understand that the motivation of the story is to have the reader consume the universe that has been created around characters or narrative, but instead as a vehicle for thoughts and ideas that the writer holds.

*ed: it’s also a form of writing that I particularly enjoy and would like to get better at, because it’s a writing style that I think I’d really enjoy to write in. In particular, I could see The Cruft existing as a piece of metafictional work that uses the musings of a hyperintelligence (currently named Hayelek) who exists in a state of observing or remarking on the phenomenon of a failed civilization that once might have existed on Earth - however the general sense that I get is that Hayelek will be more the observer while The Figure will be the one making philosophical asides - which brings our total characters in the book up to three rather than two.
