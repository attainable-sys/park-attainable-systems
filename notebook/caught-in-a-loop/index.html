Caught in a loop

1000.014 · 1000 living and loving · journal · 2023.11.13

I'm frustrated, I guess - for the most part, I feel as though I don't know what comes next for me anymore, and I'm really trying to figure that out in all the ways that I can- but I feel as though I'm caught in a loop and I don't know what it meant to come next like it's not clear to me. The dreams aren't coming anymore.
It's not as though I feel there's anything I can do about it, either. The world is shit. Most of our human experience is shit. I don't know what there is to look forward to and I can't really tell how it is that we're meant to dig our way out of it in any way other than to go through it, but the other side seems really dreary, and frustrating because I truly can't tell what you are meant to do when you get to the other side of that effort. Like, what are the dreams that people are meant to have after all of this? Are we supposed to feel as though there's any hope for a brighter future? It baffles me. I don't know how people can aspire to anything anymore. No amount of money is somehow going to make the rest of it come together and I'm watching other people put in the effort to create a life that I'm not sure I'll ever get to have. I'm 33 years old and I don't think that I have any dreams, there's this itching feeling that I'm meant to teach people, but I really can't tell if it's something that will ever happen for me because all I seem to be able to do so far is work. I'm tired, I want rest because I don't know what the other side of these feelings are. I can't see what I'm meant to do anymore, I don't really know what I'm going to do for others or the ways that I'll be able to do so because all of it is locked up against the barrier of having to think about how to make money. I'm tired of having to work like this, I'm tired of the idea that what I'm doing is something that actually means something to society when society is such bullshit, when so much of what we're dealing with is a continuous state of fear and rampage around the oppression of others. Maybe I'm just supposed to unplug, maybe I'm meant to feel disengaged.
That's probably where I'm going to end up - being disengaged feels as though it puts me in a place where I can actually think about a future without feeling as though I have to solve every problem in my present; they simply aren't my problems anymore. The other thing that comes to mind is discipline, it's the ability to get back into the mindset that I've had in the past that has yielded the results I wanted, where I was able to tell myself that there was more to my thoughts, where I was able to aspire to things. At some point, I stopped thinking like that, and I think it's because this world encourages to to feel hopeless. I'm aware of the fact that it's something I need to be responsible for, and as a result is something that I can do something about - it's just that in all the effort that I've had to make in my life to get through the experiences I've had and to become the person that I am, I'm not completely certain that I've wrapped my head around what comes next.
