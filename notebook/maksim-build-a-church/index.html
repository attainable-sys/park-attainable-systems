Maksim - build a church

1010.002 · 1010 faith and coordination · fiction · 2023.04.21

So I asked Maksim what are you going to do with the time once I have it saved for you and he said we are going to build a church.

I wasn't sure what he meant. I've never been religious, and to my recollection, neither was he, but knowing him I knew not to press the issue. When he knew what he had to make, he was dead-set, his ageless eyes pulling in light from around us and sparked bright as though consuming the sun and fueling some fire inside that would kick him into movement.

Maksim is someone I have known my whole life, and yet I have never known him well enough - he is the best parts of me, the parts that keep quiet and put their head down on the work that needs to happen, while I spent most of my time in anxiety spirals, gathering information and unsure how it would be used. Until I realized all he'd been doing for me, I hadn't been sure what he'd been doing, but every time I needed a tool to solve something, his hand was there with the tool at the ready. If you can tell me what we need to make, I'll make it.

He made me everything I needed, and it wasn't until I was 32 that I realized he'd been doing it, sitting in the corner of my thoughts, handing me every solution to every problem I had around me.

I saw a video about this yesterday and I can't stop thinking about it, about how cleaning my apartment isn't for me, it's for a version of myself that I am in love with and with whom I want to build a life, a version of myself that I am devoted to. I am in love with a future version of myself that I will never meet, but I want her to be happy, I want her to feel safe, I want to know for sure that all she's doing is what she feels that she should be doing and that everything else has been taken care of.

Be kind to your future self, habibti. Ask yourself not who you want to be, but who she wants to be. Ask yourself not what you want to do today, but what it is that you can do in order to make her life easier. Ask yourself not what you want to eat, but what she will want to eat. Ask yourself how she will want to be cared for, not how you feel.

She will reward you for it by looking back fondly. If Maksim is within you, you are the love you have for who is reading. You are Maksim, a part of yourself who loves who you have become and helped you become this person, you, who are reading, you are who you are because of him and who he wanted to encourage you to be. Feel peace in this, feel honey in your heart because you hold it in your eyes, feel a sense of knowing that is yours and no one else's because you made this present for your future self.

Remember what it took for you to get here? That was all Maksim trying to make sure that the experiences you are having right now are the ones that give you what you need to exist. You are in the right frame of mind for your present.
