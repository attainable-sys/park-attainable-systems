Welcome to nightshift radio, the unofficial radio station for all your deathmarch hour needs
We're the radio station that won't play anything at all and gives you nothing back for free, located in dark little Villar-saint-pancrace somewhere along the edge of France and italy
Tonight we'll be playing you the pattering of rain, the snoring of a teammate, and the anxiety of not knowing when the other guys show up.
Follow along as we narrate the panting of the midnight hour as a company for that red-bull you never thought you'd drink and well make that sweet sweet sensation of not knowing if it's rain or sweat dripping down your back seem normal, sleep well, just not on your feet
That's all for now, make us proud, or don't, that's fine, just don't get lost, and we'll see you in that little house down by the sea before long, or we won't, I guess we'll see you at the end of this TSP
