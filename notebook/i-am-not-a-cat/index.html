i am not a cat

1050.001 · 1050 writing · poem · 2023.03.08

I am not a cat. Maybe

i wish i was a cat because

then i'd have to force others to ask for consent

and

i could pull out my claws when

i didn't like the way they looked at me

but i think i'm better off not thinking too hard at the prospect of knowing

when someone wants to cross that divide so that

i can have them engage my more intimate

energies i

am not prepared to know what that feels like

without the experience of seeking it out.

i am not a cat because i am not

small and

i am not aloof and i am

not someone who can sit by and wait i

feel the need to move forward

into the next moment and while

cats do want to

do that, i don't think they

necessarily

do so out of a desire to want more

i am not a cat because i

want to be touched and i

want to be able to ask for it

but i don't want to ask for it

where

my dog-like tendencies tend to take over

and i don't really get a choice

around whether or not

i want someone's attention, see

a cat can fight back and be angry

and that's part of its nature

but i'm surrounded by loping paw pads

and i'm not able to move outside

without permission

maybe i am not a cat

because i have a tendency to

think that the space in between is

something shared rather than

something that is mine

and i don't have a way of sorting through

whether or not allowing it to be mine

means that i deserve it
