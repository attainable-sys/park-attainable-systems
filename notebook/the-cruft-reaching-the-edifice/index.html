The Cruft · reaching the Edifice

1050.045 · 1050 writing · fiction

Three cycles of the sun had passed and it still wasnt sure how far it had walked, the ekeing land continuous beneath its feet, wind the only real sound other than the slow movement of their footpads on a small crust below them, wind that swept across the land and through spined grasses like a boarwhistle (what was a boarwhistle, did the brain make that up) and dust grating at the surfaces of its feet, the grit building up between digits.

On the morning of the third day a black point wavered on the horizon, their eyes honing in on its shape, making out nothing, so it continued to walk under a now baking sun, the scrubs of grass culm thinned out, their shoulders flaking with sunlight, until the black point grew larger and kept growing larger, the only thing on an otherwise empty expanse, growing until it began to see that it was just as large as it had been told. Somehow the shape was unending as it began to take up the sky, pocked with openings that it began to realize were much larger than even it was, hundreds of feet off the ground and dark, the whole edifice a sharp black so stark against hoary blue sky, only slightly worn yet seeming as though age had tried to work itself against the surface.

As it drew closer and saw where this black wall extended from the ground, the wasteland was much more featured - no less barren but hiding structures below the surface that shaped the land above with little hills and valleys. It was later into the cycle; the shadows from these small hilltops dragged outward around them like the static that sometimes fills corners of their brain, a noise of imperceptible travel leading them toward the base of the monument, ending abruptly, at least from what it could see from here. The feeling was similar to images it saw when it curled up each night to rest - unsure if it was real and how far away anything was, as though it could imagine closing the distance or making shapes out of it that became something else. Moving among these long shadows felt like creeping along in sleep, until it realized that the base was approaching and the land had welled up around it, drifts of windswept landscape appearing to creep along a sheer surface careening deep into the sky above.

Well, what do I do now? The surface of the monument extended back out to the horizon to their right, and of those pockets it had seen, the nearest was hundreds of feet up the surface, divots in the face reflecting back the dull, dark glow of the sky. No way to progress from here other than to follow the surface - to pick a direction and follow it. But which way should I go? and the sun was setting lower and lower - the bitter third option to curl into resistance against a creeping cold that it had not yet grown used to in the night, this place gets so cold, and the brain began to shut down functions as they curled up into a ball against the elements, just before the infinite black mirror winked out the last bit light from the sun.

-

It walked down and over the drifts of sand piled along the smooth surface of the wall. This must be the Edifice in fact, they never told me what it looked like and the wall was long and black and forever and they walked, looking for some break in the surface, pitted but only with the wear of age and nothing else, divots no greater than pebbles in a black mirror of the desert behind them, and they kept walking along the right of the wall.

The sun rose and set and night fell and the Walker rested and the stars tinged overhead and as it walked nearly imperceptibly it seemed that the wall was shorter, and it hoped that this was true. The landscape around the wall had dulled, the short hills had grown shorter, no more scrub growing, the late sun in its setting only diffused over the features and as the Walker kept walking the land became almost flat and the wall was shorter, yes, definitely. The openings along the surface seemed closer, and as they got closer, it felt more and more as though it would find anything at all, at this point, and it kept walking because there was nothing else to do but walk and wait for the windows to come down so that it could reach up and pull itself into their ledge, so when it did it seemed like images from rest, unsure days of feeling as though the same had happened, over and over. As they kept walking the openings did seem closer, they did seem so much larger.

Endless days and then a ledge was in reach and it pulled itself up. The opening was massive, tapering slightly toward the top in a great trapezoid that seemed large enough to accommodate the sky itself, they had not seemed so large at first, and the opening receded deep into the wall and the Walker knew that this was not a wall, the ink of the opening deeper and deeper into the surface, yawning a darkness that blotted out the Walker's vision.

At what point does this become fear it thought and placed its hand on the left surface of the opening and began to walk inward because it had seen nothing so far that made it feel this. The darkness of the opening became a tunnel and the light outside faded to a pinprick quickly, it kept its hand along the left wall as nothing became visible, feeling its way further into the depth until its movement felt like nothing but floating, and the only feeling it had was the weight of its body on the soles of its feet and the cold that began to sink further into its skin and this feels pointless but there wasn't another point anyway, curiosity outweighed fear even though it didn't know if this was fear_._ Its hand slipped off the surface and into the dark and it knew there was a turn there, so it turned because somehow it knew it wouldn't, not with the Brain remembering things for it..
