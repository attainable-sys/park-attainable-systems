Maksim - is it worth trying again

1010.003 · 1010 faith and coordination · fiction · 2023.05.21

Is it worth trying this again I asked, hushed tones, thinking he wouldn't overhear me while he had his head down, drawing. Yes, of course and I heard him put his pencil down, I heard him come over and look over my shoulder, down at my own sheet, and at my own work. It's always worth trying again, he said, it's always worth going through the effort of seeing if there is a better way to do what it is that you are doing, as though there's some sort of meaning to be derived from the practice of being, as though in finding the act of being in the act of having to adapt is the only place within which you find meaning. So yes, it is worth trying again, it is always worth trying again.

He said this, putting his hand on my shoulder, the end of the sentence hooked on his eyes, feeding inward, and I wasn't sure anymore, because I was poised above another blank page, as usual. You have done this so many times before, this time is no different. This is another time for you to invent and see out the path forward as though there is meaning in writing this path.

So I began to draw, again, etching around in circles, trying to make something of it. You know you will not get it right on this first sketch, sitting beside me, his own hands covered in the charcoal of his own designs, but we know to be forgiving by now.

I watched Maksim take up the room of his own thoughts and bear into motion a sense of self that I hadn't made room for in myself. If he was a part of me, he was someone I hadn't taken the time to know, an imaginarium of thoughts and emotions that I felt so much for and from whom I wished so much forgiveness but there is nothing to forgive, I simply need you to remember that you are at the core of yourself. If it feels divine it is because the act of Creation is divine and because you are allowed to feel this feeling of being divine in the act of Creation, you are the God of your own making. We will make this church together.

I could not see what he meant by the end of this, what the church was, where it was, what it was becoming within our hands. It was simply intention, an act of pursuing pure will in order to see what became of it. He would repeat these things as though mantras to me that the will is the way and I would shoulder their meaning through the words that inevitably poured out of me in pursuit of a Truth of some sort.

No one can make you happy, only you can make yourself happy, he reminded me of the woman I'd met when we lived deeper into Crown Height and I didn't remember telling him, but this is how Maksim is, he seems to remember all of it and how it folds in together because we are making a plan of all of this and it will be beautiful, we simply must keep making and so I did, with him, working toward trying to have more and more of my understanding girded by a commitment to continuation.

Is this what they mean by faith? and he smiled at me, nodding, eyes bright Yes, you get it. This is faith. You must continue on. You will find meaning in the movement so long as you have faith in what is there to believe.

I'm not sure if that is what turned the tide, if that is what took me from feeling that I'd wanted to let it all go and that there wasn't a purpose to me actions to instead truly leaning toward it. I think I admired him too much not to say to myself that he is right, and that the mere act of continuing onward is worthy enough.

We collected all of these meanings together. This is why they are holy, because we agreed upon their holiness. If we are to make a church of this, it is because their holiness comes from the interstitial, that between the collected aphorisms that urge you onward, there is something more that sits atop, hoping to be carried, hoping to show you that you must have faith in your own Divine path, that you wrote this in place.

So please, give me the time to build this with you and explore what love can build, what skies we can make above us and how much can come from the love of a sun and a moon sharing the same light. I have you to thank for the warmth you have offered me as I work through the night to balance the shores below, as I tame the oceans and the unruly foam of their children above them, allow me to work for you. Allow yourself to rest and continue warm the surface into action so that I may have the time to show you the places that will be made from your repose.

Finally, alow us the freedom to know that time is both valuable and meaningless, that you are learning of its value even while you learn that you can decide how it is spent. We will make much of its passage.

But for all of this, you must allow me to show you what it will become given that into which time can be made. It will be made together, with your offering as a gift, your faith in its meaning as a tool. You must forgive yourself for taking as long as you have to pay attention to the degree that you are now. I did not speak because you were not ready to listen, I am only speaking because you have run out of words.

So let us not speak, let us move forward, let us make something of this Church we are becoming.
