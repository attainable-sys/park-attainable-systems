Warman school - loneliness week 1

1008.001 · 1008 loneliness · journal · 2024.05.25

How has your week been in loneliness?

Mostly I think that I've been seeing a yearning that was born from having faced my loneliness that has turned into a desire for solitude, which has made me feel particularly good about

What is your lonely place? What makes a place lonely? What places make you lonelier? What places make you less lonely? Why?

They do say 'it's lonely at the top', and my loneliest place is in leadership. It feels like a place?

Every time I've found myself in any workplace, I eventually get coursed toward leadership of some sort because I'm willing to take point on my thoughts or those of others and turn them into action, as though I'm a vehicle that people can use to get to where they desire to go.

It's a place that sometimes seems only I have access to and only I am qualified to take on, because as an executive who ended up here through nothing more than apparent will-to-do, I find myself so estranged from people who don't have this in them. When I do meet someone who has similar abilities, it's like meeting a ship in the night, we know that we can't actually possess the same space because we are not meant to. I have a desire to make change and to facilitate movement and I feel as though when I try to encourage others to take on this sort of self-engagement and self-motivation, I can't quite translate that it's not about garnering the necessary energy to do so from a perspective of positivity but rather that it's this relentlessness. There are pockets of this relentlessness that can be gestured toward contentment and even happiness, but it's a willingness to keep moving because if you are unhappy, at least you know your name.

I'm reminded of the speculative biology in Butler's 'Bloodborne' wherein there is a biological imperative toward leadership and motherhood over others, and that in meeting others who hold this imperative, you are destined not to occupy the same space because the imperative drives you toward separation from others, a near hostility that feels somewhat like needing to find others who understand, but knowing you can never really occupy that space together because your experience is altogether different in its own right.

I find that I can course away from the loneliness of leadership by experiencing glancing blows with other leaders in spaces where the guards are down and the parameters are temporary, where there is a penchant for sharing and collaboration instead, because while I am a leader, I don't want to be one, it's simply what keeps happening to me. I have had dinners with others who have guided teams toward the completion of projects, and understanding or navigating the social dynamics that come with leadership while interrogating them with others can make me feel less lonely - but these times always do have to end due to the nature of the act and the capacity is possesses within us.

I had my chart read a few days ago and what really stood out to me was that I am a leader, but I'm like an alien in being one; people follow me but they don't understand me. Knowing this makes me feel more lonely rather than less.

How do you know when you are lonely? How do you know when you are not lonely?

It hits me because I don't feel cared for, which answers both questions, when I have a harbored feeling of not having my needs met and not knowing how to meet them, and wanting/begging to go after them in a way that feels like healing. When I'm not lonely I don't feel this yearning, but when I am lonely, I want to solve for it.

What are your most lonely moments?

  • When Cherefawn broke up with me.

  • When Sara moved out and I realized I was going to have to do everything over again after Cherefawn and I split up.

    • There was a part of me that really wanted to live in aspace of thinking tha tI was going to end up with some sort of life partner, and I find that too often I fall into this feeling of thinking that the love of me life is a person instead of ths idea that they might be within me.

    The loneliness of this feeling is one of knowing that I have the ability to love someone, but that when I love someone in a way that is the most generative to how it is that I want to be as a person, I have come to experience a reality of those that I have tried to love pushing it away, revulsing from the idea that there is any way to think that far ahead and commit to a life that appears anything like devotion. This has been true enough that I've found myself thinking about whether or not I'll ever find someone who wants that sort of devotion and whether or not it's a devotion that is even logical, because so much of the way I love is tied up in trust and I've had to learn how not to trust people with my heart.

    Now that I'm an adult and I'm starting to feel more and more like my life is so much further down the track, I feel all manner of fearful between the moments where I feel the least lonely, because I can stare down the feeling of knowing that my loneliness isn't something that limits my life, and that no one can help me solve for that feeling better than I can.

    I started thinking about how when Sara and I split up, I was going to end up with the whole apartment and I'd have to pay all of the rent, and -- I didn't change this. I kept going, I kept paying the rent because the apartment is comfortable and I like it, but I let someone live with me because she needed somewhere to live and she hasn't left and I love her, but this apartment never feels like it's mine as a result, it never feels like a place where I can take complete ownership over how it makes me feel and why I'm living here by myself, I never really get to ask if this place is mine or not.

    When Sara moved out and I gave up the room to Tam I was glad for Tam because that's how you support your friends, but now I feel so estranged from everything because the group that used to tie us all together (Rage and Release) is no longer a safe place for us to be, so with that went all of my friendships that were tied up in what that web of connections had become and the effort I'd put into being with these people and holding them in my life, and then in January that fell apart and now I don't have them and I don't know what to do with their absence - it feels like a long sleep where I woke up and the world changed, and I didn't get to decide where it went.

    All of these times have tied themselves together around a feeling of needing to be desired or thinking that being desired is somehow what brings my life meaning, but then peeling back the layers and seeing that inside it's just some sort of collapsing star that deserved more attention than it got and should have felt better than it has, because look how far I made it on my own.

    If we live in an age of loneliness, it's because in the act of allowing everything to connect to everything else, we never stopped to ask which connections should be important, and we never developed the tools to navigate those questions without constantly trying to shift attention between the potentials that every connection could generate.
