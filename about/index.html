About

I'm Park Feierbach. I photograph, write, and study systems — the kind that emerge from networks, from ecologies, from people building things together. The throughline: how does information move through a system, and what does it change?

Anthropology (UGA, 2012) → photographer & archivist (National Geographic, three books) → Protocol Research (Figment) → Community Research (Namada) → CEO, Radiant Commons (Penumbra) → writing

I am dedicated to stories. What I do is examine what is happening and help people understand it.

Writing my novel is the most meaningful thing I have ever done, even though it is not finished. Los Cielos Desaparecen.

The long version, in forty slides: ¿a qué te dedicas?

The name pantalaimon is a daemon who never settled. This site is hand-coded, tracks nothing, and mirrors the structure of the vault it grew from.
