Per [[Kundera, M]], elliptical writing is the act of not-writing everything that you intend to have as a part of your text, the you should indeed leave some of the imagining up to interpretation on the part of the reader.

It's for this reason that [[slice-of-life]] style writing is a useful vehicle for introducing people to stories - rather than completely covering an idea end-to-end, there's a way for the writing to encompass only that which is directly relevant so that there's a way for the reader to expand what exists beyond what they have written on their own.

In fact, this is one of the more powerful notions when it comes to writing, to give your reader something that they can interpret and build out, and further is the very reason that it's valuable to flesh everything out as a writer, but then only write about what is important.

Elliptical writing has us drop into a story and fill in the rest of the characters and setting with our own imagination, a way of building something beautiful out of the idea of beauty itself, rather than the literal imposition of beauty upon the observer.
