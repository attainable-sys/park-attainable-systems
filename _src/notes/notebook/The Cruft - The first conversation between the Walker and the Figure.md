"the distraction of knowing how time treats us, it is not unfair or cruel, it is observant, and in so being sees us in a Knowing that we can control the time we are allotted and the ways in which it is used."

the stories of this Earth are long past. they are memories, and as with most memories, they are incomplete. in the time where everything happened, The habit was to remember it all, the dream that what should be as a catalog of all that was - but we know - you know - that Wonder comes from incomplete things, from the open-ended questions that pour forth from mystery. they in their time obsessed over memory instead of giving value to the passage of time and the sheer magic of proving its existence. some knew this and built cultures and traditions of [[forgetting]], and we can honor them as much as we can honor those who chose to remember it all. those who chose to forget allowed those few to move on - to know that which is valuable from the past and to move forward in its memory, but not in an exacting reflection of the past. you cannot replace that which is already happened, because now is a far more powerful message than then. even you know this, to take what you have learned and build the steps needed toward the future instead. it is a dream caught in the act of its own realization, and now you know you can be present for the event.

I cannot remember this place for you. even the memories I hold are fragmented, or somehow less than what was expected - but I am as well better for it. but I remember is harrowing and has crushed me, and the years have removed of it it's edges and the fear of its repetition - but the sun is still glorious. I still see it's light as something that gives back. I remember hope and time has given me an appreciation for that which I don't remember. what I don't remember is what makes the legend. what I've seen since then, and what I've retained - it told me that the most unstoppable of forces is this forward movement. I have found rust in appreciating its passage and reconfiguration. every moment can pour forth gifts."

[[Figure]] sat on nothing, breathing the facsimile of exhaustion from a shimmering blue of having taken in passing ages.

"but you can see all of this for yourself. there is meaning here, should you seek it. I cannot say that I think knowing the story as it occurred will be worth your effort, but it may be, maybe the story you learn will inspire you to seek out more. I hope this for you, I encourage you, and when you have gone and seen all that you can learn, come back to me, and I will tell you what I do remember, and I will tell you that it's beautiful, and I will tell you why I think it is beautiful."
