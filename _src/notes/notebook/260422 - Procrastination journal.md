I did it again, I sat in my living room playing video games until 11 in the morning and never actually got my day started, and now I'm writing my journal entry here on this computer instead of in the notebook that I know needs to contain these thoughts, and this computer is about to die. It's been telling me since about an hour ago, and since, all I've done is watch Instagram videos, I started watching a video about how to have a system for the sort of effectiveness that I aspire to have.
So what is effectiveness, if that's really what I'm aiming for? That's the real question I've had to ask myself, because I don't seem to be able to figure it out. According to [this video](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DXVHU7gDei6/), it's the work of figuring out the following:
- S - Specific,
	- don't be vague, nail down the tolerances.
- Y - Yours
	- build it for YOUR life, not a general one. You have to be in charge of what you are doing.
- S - Scheduled
	- not scheduled means optional. It means you don't really need to do it if you don't feel like it. If it's on a schedule, you have to.
- T - Trackable
	- Make it so that you can actually measure the progress you are making and improve on it. This is **crucial**.
- E - Easy to execute
	- All the steps of the process should by tiny
- M - Maintained
	- Repeat it constantly.

The issue I see with all of these videos is that too much of the time I look at them and I don't know what to think other than 'here's something that I have to consume that will eventually lead me to further destroy myself. At some point I had a schedule, but then when I wake up and I feel like shit, or I didn't actually do what I intended to do for the day, I find that too often I'm caught in a loop. I don't end up caring, because I'm not actually accountable to anyone, and no one really cares whether or not I am. That's the hardest part of doing anything for yourself, and I know I know it, because it's something that I've had to encounter any number of times before.
So what does this mean: I do need a system. I need something that I can lean on that shows me what I'm doing and how I'm doing it. Everything leading up to my graduate school applications made sense because it was something that I could depend on, and it was measurable. I didn't set things in motion just because. I was training for capability. I came out of it somewhat injured, and I don't think I did what I needed to in order to condition myself for the sort of performance that I wanted to have. I was sold short on myself, even though I still saw out what I wanted to do, at whatever speed it took to do it. I can do that again. My running schedule is something that has to come back, because it's part of the backbone of making sure the rest of it happens.
I also need to be strict again. It doesn't matter if I wake up tired, I need to wake up when I intend to, not when I'm ready to. That's a difference from how I've been doing things for the last few weeks: I need to wake up at the same time, earlier than I'm doing it. My dog deserves it, I deserve it. As I write this, it's eleven, and I'm starting my day. Let's not have too many more of those. I'm depressed. That's okay. I need rest to recuperate and figure some things out. That's also okay. That doesn't mean that we can't see out what we expect from it all, and it doesn't mean I can't aim higher.
As I write this, my nervous system is coming back to rest. I'm allowing my unconscious to be drawn back into what I need to be doing rather than what I've been doing by default. I have calls today that I need to prepare for. I have the ability to execute on a dream and do more than I'm giving myself the credit of needing to do. This book needs to go through the hard part and I need to see what happens. I started working this morning, then I stopped. That still needs to happen, but only after I do the following:

We're going to be okay. I need to come up with the guardrails that ensure I succeed. It's better to work on the things we care about than to be stuck in the same loops of frustrated detachment.
