So here is the best part of it all, the dream that I felt as a result of having looked back, when I was on that beach and I was spending time with all of these new friends that I'd made on the beach: Aaron had just left and was on the way out to Quito to fly home for Christmas, while I was planning on spending that time with a constellation of people I'd met on the beach - people I'd worked on a farm with and with whom I'd formed friendships.

I can't really remember the people that I met but I remember that we went down to the beach to look for fish so that we could have them with dinner, and we made a feast around the different kinds of fish that I was able to buy from a fisherman on the shore who had caught them that morning - we put them on spits and cooked them, then shared them in an open yard with one another, until finally we all left to wander down toward the beach together.

There wasn't a lot for us to do other than drink with one another, but I wanted to get a raging bonfire going, so I got everyone to go with me up the shoreline to find wood for what we wanted to have around us as we spent time together. Once enough had been gathered, everyone was well drunk, and we were enjoying the sense of lost time that comes with passage of other people's lives around you, I remember my friend turning to me and saying _you can relax now, you made all of this happen_ all while allowing me to sit back and enjoy the cachaça that was brought by the brazilians.

I remember even now that in those times there was a lot of liberty in being able to live a life like that - I've spent a healthy number of christmasses without my family and it's mostly because I don't think the idea of christmas is something that needs celebrating. It's a nice holiday, but it's not exactly meaningful without there being religious to back it, because I don't think what the point of it is becomes easily discerned without knowing this reasoning, so I don't think that there really is a point.

So what can I learn from this story? What is it that I think proves something about me and makes it so that it's clear I know how to do what I know I know how to do as a result of telling this story> I t means that I can create community, it means that I can bring people together, and it means that alongside all of these other skills, I know how to keep people happy, I know how to tell stories, and I know how to offer to others the creation of a space that is well shared and comfortable. That's always been true of me, as hard as it can get sometimes.

The worst is that now I'm really not even waking up in the morning to go to work. I'm really just... phoning in in at this point. There are no deliverables, so I have nothing to deliver, and I have no way of trying to get myself to move froward a state where that's something that I do.

I'm so tired, I really feel as though I'm about to fall asleep in and around this. The point is that on that beach, I felt something that I haven't felt many times in my life- the power of being able to bring people together. Because I was able to speak multiple languages, I was able to get everyone on the same page, and I was able to coordinate us all around the resolution that needed o happen in order to best optimize the situation for the greatest possible group benefit. I don't know if that's something that I should feel proud of, but it's definitely something that I care about a lot so I'm trying to think about what the right way of approaching it is, so here we are.
