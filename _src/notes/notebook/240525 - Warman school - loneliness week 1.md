### What is your lonely place? What makes a place lonely? What places make you lonelier? What places make you less lonely? Why?

They do say 'it's lonely at the top', and my loneliest place is in leadership. It feels like a place?

Every time I've found myself in any workplace, I eventually get coursed toward leadership of some sort because I'm willing to take point on my thoughts or those of others and turn them into action, as though I'm a vehicle that people can use to get to where they desire to go.

It's a place that sometimes seems only I have access to and only I am qualified to take on, because as an executive who ended up here through nothing more than apparent will-to-do, I find myself so estranged from people who don't have this in them. When I do meet someone who has similar abilities, it's like meeting a ship in the night, we know that we can't actually possess the same space because we are not meant to. I have a desire to make change and to facilitate movement and I feel as though when I try to encourage others to take on this sort of self-engagement and self-motivation, I can't quite translate that it's not about garnering the necessary energy to do so from a perspective of positivity but rather that it's this relentlessness. There are pockets of this relentlessness that can be gestured toward contentment and even happiness, but it's a willingness to keep moving because if you are unhappy, at least you know your name.

I'm reminded of the speculative biology in Butler's 'Bloodborne' wherein there is a biological imperative toward leadership and motherhood over others, and that in meeting others who hold this imperative, you are destined not to occupy the same space because the imperative drives you toward separation from others, a near hostility that feels somewhat like needing to find others who understand, but knowing you can never really occupy that space together because your experience is altogether different in its own right.

I find that I can course away from the loneliness of leadership by experiencing glancing blows with other leaders in spaces where the guards are down and the parameters are temporary, where there is a penchant for sharing and collaboration instead, because while I am a leader, I don't want to be one, it's simply what keeps happening to me. I have had dinners with others who have guided teams toward the completion of projects, and understanding or navigating the social dynamics that come with leadership while interrogating them with others can make me feel less lonely - but these times always do have to end due to the nature of the act and the capacity is possesses within us.

I had my chart read a few days ago and what really stood out to me was that I am a leader, but I'm like an alien in being one; people follow me but they don't understand me. Knowing this makes me feel more lonely rather than less.

### How do you know when you are lonely? How do you know when you are not lonely?

It hits me because I don't feel cared for, which answers both questions, when I have a harbored feeling of not having my needs met and not knowing how to meet them, and wanting/begging to go after them in a way that feels like healing. When I'm not lonely I don't feel this yearning, but when I am lonely, I want to solve for it.
