There are times where i can feel it roaring
Like rage inside of me but
Not thinking as though
It is a notion to attend nor
Is the weight of it
Something that brings me to the closeness
I have earned.

There are times where I have turned myself over
To the notion that
Things do not need to get better
And that there is something holy
Made from knowing oneself
From confidently stripping away
The sea of worry
That engulfs our souls
On the way to self knowledge

We have earned the keep
The way of becoming oneself
That accompanies
Not having wavered
Atterizaje
The same words uttered over and over,
In the feeling of respecting ones own death echo
As it reverberated backward through time into the present

Grace, I have known you
Through the hoary weight of time
I have feared the same things
As those that weigh you down.

Grace, I have known you
Hearing and respecting
In whatever waveforms have caused me
To come to shore in and among the sands
Of a beach I call home

And I am being called homeward

000

Grace I have known you hearing me
In the best of times
Not having thought that this was a place
That I would stop calling home
Grace
I thought this would be home
And so I stretched myself across it
As though it would be such a place
I bored into the landscape
With the desire to sink into it
And feel the moss of what its age had to offer.

Now I am lucky to know something for sure
In exchange for what it gave me;
A family that I have heard all too well
Telling me that I am loved
unwavered by ocean
or coastline

000

I think I heard far too late
The diminishing echo
Of knowing I had to make sense of my time
So my reaction when it was made louder
By the canyon walls I chose to be surrounded by
It was not something to which I was ready to react

But it enveloped me
and i heard it
And knew better then than I had before
That i am not home
And I am looking for it
And so are you

Grace, give me mention
For at least the time I spent wandering
In places that offered me
 The unbridled joy
Into which I sank
Grace I know better where home is now but
I also know better that it is not a place i will find wandering

Settled into my sorrow
I know I brought you here to let pass
the empty gravel of searching
The return to my roots
The emptying of bookcases and living rooms and
Meals and places
I wanted more of and
haven’t mentioned yet missing.
