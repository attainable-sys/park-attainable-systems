"That's because it wasn't - this is is just how I remembered it. I remembered it as an explosion of hues, of reds, blues, of watching the waves and my feet getting wet, of thinking through what it meant to be somewhere that I'm not. The reason it looks like this is because this is how I want it to be remembered."
