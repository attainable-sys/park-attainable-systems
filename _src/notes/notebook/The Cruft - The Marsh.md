The [[Walker]] moved through a marsh, feeling its way though sodden ground, hesitant. The ground seemed to pull at its feet, sucking down the weight of its body, each step was a thread. On the morning of the second day,, it found a clear pool of water, green across its surface with small leaves, a kind of plant that was growing ion the surface and across it, and in the middle a corona of rings with a tendrilled thing at the center. The ring extended out from this center wide, an heavier looking than it must have been, {stamen? this word came to Mind} and in the breeze the Walker watched the object float slightly to the left, the solid, foamy collar floating on the surface of the pond.
It watched a while, walked further, then a rustle in the reeds stopped it, leg half-lifted into the next step, and a four legged, furry thing hopped from copse to copse, oblivious to the [[Walker]], its head (is that what it was?) down the top of each tuft, a small pink tongue peeking out to lick the place where reeds came together
