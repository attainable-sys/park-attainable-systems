There's nothing wrong with starting over, I tell myself, knowing that it isn't true and that at best I'm going to have to work through the details of a negative experience. There's nothing wrong with waking up in a different city, thinking mostly about the past and not really conspiring about the future.

There's an electricity in the air regardless - that part of the idea is trying to move an idea forward so that as much of everything can be implicated from the silence that surrounds it, and that there isn't much that can be done from questioning value on the way to getting there. It's cold in Paris, my fingers are having a hard time putting the letters into motion, I miss my dog.

Even the while, knowing that there is direction given to what it means to proceed and expecting that there will be even more in motion, we listen to the silence as though it's telling us something, as though the next step of construction will come from something other than popularization of a few concepts and phrases, rather than any sort of expectation that there's something to be done anout the reality that surrounds us. II'm not sure what the expetation is, but we'll have to pretend that we're going to get there when we get there. I'm not sure sure that's how things work.

If it does, then there's a lot for people to think about in the means of getting to wherever they expect that they will go. Too much language, too little anger about the state of things, too little adaptation. If the movement is neutral, then there will be no passion.

So what is the language to be used around expectation? What is the means through which solutions can be landed upon? How do you explain the route that must be taken, how do you land in a place that makes sense of the present, how do you overrule the silence of the unknown.

The reason that these things, in motion, require a heedlessness, is an attention toward recognizing that it must be done without thinking. It must be done without expecting that there's a sense to it all, and it must be done without expecting that the solution can and will be made from moving onward. Half of the work that needs to be done, at the least. Half of the expectation. Half of the assumption, half of the action. And what good would that do? How do you arrive in a place that makes sense of an expectation and meets it with a sequence of meanings and actions that do not know how to arrive in this place? The best things were made without planning for them, and because of this must be achieved without expecting that they will be made. What does this mean for everything that can be arrived at through intention? Are they possible?

I'm unsure. I think uncertainty is a state of being. We're better off for existing within is, but we are also better off for believing that we must have faith in the power of movement, that weight will be lifted through this. May be we are better off communicating this reality to the exist that is required of believing that we are made up of our actions, that we are the metaplex of beliefs and compound intentions that make up the emergent state that exists on top of history. We are made up of mistakes, of half-truths, of things that arrive where they must be without being appreciated for what they were made into.

This being true, what does it mean, really, to have to listen to the sound of unmet challenges. We have to take them head-on and we have to believe that addressing what is possible will be met with what can be invented in the moment, that expectations are explained from the likelihood that expectation is explicable.

Take no mindshare from the present, believe full well that the future can be made from what is expected. It's easier to believe that we can make it through whatever unknown we can believe exists than to believe that we have to sit and ponder possibility. The latter is a waste of emotional state, but adapting continuously to the realization that something is wrong with the current approach is a very real risk, and it needs to be taken in, because it's the most cancerous part of the problem - knowing that you cannot reach what is necessary through the mere believe that the present will simply solve for the path. There's also a need to be reasonable.

So I don't know - take what you will from these words. Know that there's something to be said within them that is meant to inspire you into action, but that none of it is meant to scare you, like winter coming forth to expect hope from spring. Accomplishing what is necessary to reach whatever is next comes from a parade of unknowing, from a fear of meaninglessness. Don't think like that. Think instead of what it means to create something that everyone will not only want but will also need. The challenge is in knowing what that thing is.
