They unlocked the door to the interrogation room and there, sitting in the provided chair, was a slight woman, eyes a little bit cagey, but striking. Dark black hair, a strong, curved nose, dark skin in the way of leather heavy, driven eyes staring right through his own. Something about them - not quite a fire - more a question of knowing, something powerful that seemed to bely a knowledge. Wizened, but tired. He extended his hand.

"Well alright, hello, my name is Detective Harrow. I'm with the Federal Bureau of Investigations. I'm not sure how you were able to convince the people who called me, but I hear that you're here to talk about magic? What was your name?"
She looked back at him, her eyes sparking.
"My name is Leta."
"Ok, nice to meet you Leta. I apologize for all the formality to do with making you wait. However, you're quite persistent and you've been really patient, so I'm glad to make the time here. How can we help you?"
"My people are dying. No one knows, this needs to change."
He looked at her, pausing.
"Ma'am, have you had a drug test?"
She didn't react.
"Have you taken any medication today?"
A moment passed before she looked up at him, opening a mouth that didn't belong in cities and looked instead like someone who had spent years in the sun.
"My people are dying. I did not want to come here. I did not want to do this. I did not want to speak with you and show you what has happened but we cannot fix this on our own and we must fix it."
