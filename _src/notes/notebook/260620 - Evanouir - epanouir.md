As you watch gold leave
From the crown of the trees
On something street
Through the skylight of our car
Three things will come to mind:
You will watch two birds, one airplane, a crow
A fly
The pink of long lightbeams in
The clouds beyond
Two words in french;
Evanouir: to grow faint, to pass out, to vanish
And epanouir: to flourish, to grow, into something more
And I turn to you
