I think life is a consequence
I think life is a consequence of having tried to become more than the dust we were born to be, that we are consciousness that emerged from the universe considering too deeply whether or not there is purpose
I think life is a consequence and because of it we are tasked with the frustration of living, of coming up against the knowledge that only we can create meaning, and that when these atoms of dust came together to become who we are, we were already destined to fail
I think life is a consequence but I think this consequence is beautiful, and because of the dust we're made from we're required to consider the irrelevance of our existence, that these atoms of dust that gathered in the storm of possibility are something more than just dust, they are beautiful and we are beautiful
but I think that this consequence is a cost incurred, it's something that we'll always carry as a burden, always forced into the act of living as though a rebellion against the entropy of time, and I think that consequence is something we'll carry until our dying breath

until out dying breath
