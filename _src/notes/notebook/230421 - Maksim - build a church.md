So I asked Maksim _what are you going to do with the time once I have it saved for you_ and he said _we are going to build a church._

I wasn't sure what he meant. I've never been religious, and to my recollection, neither was he, but knowing him I knew not to press the issue. When he knew what he had to make, he was dead-set, his ageless eyes pulling in light from around us and sparked bright as though consuming the sun and fueling some fire inside that would kick him into movement.

Maksim is someone I have known my whole life, and yet I have never known him well enough - he is the best parts of me, the parts that keep quiet and put their head down on the work that needs to happen, while I spent most of my time in anxiety spirals, gathering information and unsure how it would be used. Until I realized all he'd been doing for me, I hadn't been sure what he'd been doing, but every time I needed a tool to solve something, his hand was there with the tool at the ready. _If you can tell me what we need to make, I'll make it_.

He made me everything I needed, and it wasn't until I was 32 that I realized he'd been doing it, sitting in the corner of my thoughts, handing me every solution to every problem I had around me.
