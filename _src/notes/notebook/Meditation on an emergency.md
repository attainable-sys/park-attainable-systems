fend for the little space you still have fight for and I hope you win
I hope no one ever takes it from you and in its place puts a vacuum, a tree under which the weight of its branches causes the city to come by and cut it down, a hole in the ground and in the open sky that reminds you there used to be something here

in this storefront
that used to be a vacant lot
that's now closed
or condos
built, standing
I don't know
Two hundred fucking feet call, stealing the skyline from the window you used to look out of
and stayed there
waiting
to come clean
in the traffic
out of business (mind your business)
for someone
just like me

do you see it?
I remember
what this storefront

used to be
