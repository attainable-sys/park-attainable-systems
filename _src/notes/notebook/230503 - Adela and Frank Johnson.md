I think I'd be

better off

not telling you

everything that went on in my head

i think

i'd be better off telling you

that there's more in here

than you're aware of

and that i'm not going to be

able to hold it all in

you see i'm

not really in charge

of whatever it is that

i'm feeling

i'm just sort of

happening to me

and i have lately

been feeling a lot of things

lately

that have me thinking

that i'd rather

not fall in love again

but also a lot of things that

i know mean

i don't get to make that decision

so the part of me that broke

in front of you

last night

was somehow not

something i deserve to hold

because honestly

i'm just not in charge of it

and i keep breaking it

over and over

i keep thinking

that if i hold the pieces in

just the right way

they will somehow

hold on to the light.

i don't intend for you to hear this

just so that you're somehow more afraid

of what i'm saying,

this isn't meant to be fearful

and i know it's too much

because again

it's just not fair when

so much can happen so quickly

and you can have so little to do

with whether or not

you wanted it to

but

i know that sharing something

with a perfect stranger,

is a rare type of kindness,

something i'm prepared to do and

something that i wasn't taught to

and i just

didn't really expect to go through that with you

i don't think i would have

had you not stopped too

i don't think it would have been

so precious, and kind

and i don't think i would really have

been able to prove to you

that i didn't mean for it to happen

other than it happening

to the two of us at the same time

you see i got home and i just

sat down on the floor and cried

i cried so hard

and you still don't know that i did

all you saw was after he left to catch the bus

all you saw was me not being able to hold on

right in that moment

and

then when i regained composure i

was able to still sit there

and hear so much about you that

i'm unsure if you knew you wanted to share

but we just kept going

and somehow i still didn't feel like i could tell you

the next day

that yeah

when i got home,

i cried on the floor of my hallway

for ten minutes

for a perfect stranger

who didn't have control over their life.

i cried and didn't have control of myself

because i just don't think any of this matters

as much as i keep telling myself it can.

too much of this isn't

that important

too much of this is

fragile and relative

and easy to lose context on

because you don't get to choose

when or where something can change you forever,

because most of the time

you don't get to choose

what part of living is

something that happens in front of other people

so imagine with me that

we at least have control over this

and when i told you that when we'd part ways

i'd want to kiss you you

didn't really respond, you

just smiled

and you didn't know that i only got

three blocks away before i started crying

i cried because of course in an ideal world

the art form of getting to design an interaction

makes it so that

learning constantly about someone else

means

learning that you like the texture of their chapped lips

means

hurting in private

because you had told yourself

that you didn't want to love again

means

getting to hold at arms length

the whole of your love
