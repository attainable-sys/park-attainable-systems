## Vibes:
- We want good vibes
	- Celebrate the wins,
		- 50/100/150/etc
- I wear my emotions on my face
	- When I am non verbal, I'll use hand signals to tell you how I'm feeling
	- I don't want to be asked over and over how I'm doing
- Medchecks are important, that's something that I do know I need
- I don't like mouth noises
- I welcome running with people, but I don't want people behind me, it makes me feel pursued
	- Lead me or run beside me
- I don't want math - don't tell me how much time I have left, tell me how much longer I need to run
- Music is helpful
