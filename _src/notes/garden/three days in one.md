1. **The first part of the day is from 5-11** - this is a time for using energy and being creative, for doing things like writing, thinking, exploring, running, etc. This is a good time to be with oneself.
2. **The second part of the day is from 11-5** - this is a time for meeting with people and deciding on what we want to have happen, or it is a time where checking in should happen. It's something that requires a maximal amount of effort in terms of engaging with people and expending this energy, so it's a time that should not have many of these meetings. Remember, it's possible to take your time with how you approach things.
3. **The third part of the day is from 5-11** - this is a time for study and consuming information that is beneficial to the mind, it's a time for building on previous knowledge and becoming a better version of oneself, and a time to draw conclusions.
