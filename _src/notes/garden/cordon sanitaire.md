en bref - the cordon sanitaire is an intellectual or sociological barrier around that which can and should and that which can't and shouldn't be spoken or thought of. The origins of this concept exist in Belgian social theory, and were conceived around such concepts that are societally deleterious - whether as [[taboo]] or as an agreed-upon [[boundary condition]] of concept that are not intellectually useful to investigate, because they are [[toxic logic]] that is actively dangerous to think about.

In some ways, the cordon sanitaire is a [[boundary condition]] necessary to defend against the [[meme|memetic]] qualities of [[antimeme]]s, taking out the legs necessary for societally deleterious concepts such as [[eugenics]] and its effect on justifying and fortifying [[racism]]. If it is not explored, it is not expanded - if it is not expanded, it remains small.
