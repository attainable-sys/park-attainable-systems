An open primitive is a component of a system that functions as a building block for other parts of the system while not positioning itself as an extractive resource. Fundamentally. they are subsidized by the structure of the system itself and have collaborative relationships with their surroundings by means of which they can both be sustained and sustain the [[system]]s they are a part of.
