The term "small-but-mighty" refers to the concept of highly-connected individual nodes in a network that act as information highways for local clusters. These nodes have a low degree of randomness and high clustering, making them crucial for network resilience. They play a significant role in maintaining strong long-distance connections across shorter path lengths, leading to small-world connectivity. The term emphasizes the importance of these well-connected individuals in the stability and resilience of the network.
