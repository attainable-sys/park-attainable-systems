## Summary
Proofsquare is a workflow automation tool created by [[ContributionDAO]] that allows you to connect things that you need to do across difference aspects of your community as it grows.

 connecting to different dApps - integrates with a number of different applications.
	- Anything in other languages can be put together to connect to other protocols, then can be turned into content that can be utilized across applications
	- Creation of workflows
		- i.e. AMA > Translation > Approvals > etc.
