Candor is being frank withy our criticism, but offering a road to improvement so that someone can get back on track. Candor requires that you offer someone the emotional space to criticize you, and *always in private*.

- -> Say 'no' more often than you say yes, so that you can help people refine their process.
- -> Look for pathways toward both reaching and seeing the results you're looking for
- -> Care personally, challenge directly
