The Karadshev Scale represents the levels of progression through which society may optimally utilize locally available energy. The scale proceeds as follows:
- Type 1 - The civilization has taken full advantage of all energy that hits their planet
- Type 2 - The civilization has taken full advantage of all energy released by the star in it's solar system.
- Type 3 - The civilization has taken full advantage of all possible stars available in it's neighborhood, or entire galaxies, white holes, and gamma ray bursts.

Interestingly, humanity is unlikely to reach Kardashev scale level 1 any time in the near future and is currently a solid Kardashev type 0 civilization.
