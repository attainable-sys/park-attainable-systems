Department structure for leadership:

| Role           | Concern                    | Focus        |
| -------------- | -------------------------- | ------------ |
| Product Owner  | What do we build           | User         |
| Project Lead   | Build it on time           | Coordination |
| Technical Lead | Build it well              | Tech         |
| Steward        | Make sure people are happy | People       |

Stewardship is about ensuring that people feel that they are working on what they want to work on --> not management, stewardship.
