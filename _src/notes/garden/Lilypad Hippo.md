Lilypad Hippos are a form of [[speculative biology]] that evolved in a post-[[Cruft event]] and post [[Spore Earth]] landscape, and a native animal that has evolved as part of [[The Cruft]] ecosystem.

Briefly defined, they are a marginally sightless quadruped using a wide collar of fungus to help the animal afloat on the surface of bodies of water. In younger nymph stage, a [[Lilypad Hippo]] can walk around without the fungal collar impeding movement, but as these hippos age, the collar grows beyond the circumference of their legs, leading a hippo eventually remaining in a floating state, with the collar continuing to grow as fungal roots sprout from the hippo corpus further into the body of the water.
