Utopiary is a hypothetical DAO that aiming to align consumer incentives with societal intentions.

### What would it take for this to work?
It would take people who have the technical expertise to construct efficient tokenomics, as well as run validators for the organization. As a result, the organization would have a continuous flywheel of income through which to contribute to public goods funding, so that the treasury of goods would be on a constant but not exponential growth curve.

This would allow continuous funding of public goods through profitable means to those who contribute / stake to the organization's validators, in turn guaranteeing a positive return for staking involvement on the part of those who contribute to the pool.
### How would it make money?
Running a green validator: the validator takes 15% say commission on validation, 50% of which goes to operation costs, the other 50% of which goes to green incentives. Best part is, stakers still get 100% of the rewards that they deserve from the validator **+** a continuous drip of tokens from Utopiary.
#### $UTO tokens
Utopiary is run by both the community members and it's employees. Roles are determined through utility, and paid out in the form of $UTO tokens. The $UTO tokens can then be staked as a pair
### What is the point of it?
The point of Utopiary is to keep organizations within the intentions of what the broadest collective would prefer (i.e. reducing environmental impact) by creating an incentive mechanism around doing social good, based on what it is that people in the public want to do.

The organization would be committed to being not only carbon neutral, but carbon negative, while still being profitable for those who work within the organization.

### Why is this exciting?
Too often, organizations that can have a positive social impact are kneecapped by the need to make a profit, or exist within granting systems. The point of this organization would be to pool the funds received from other liquidity influxes into the largest possible treasury allocation toward the funding of that which is deemed socially necessary.

### Where would it deploy?

Ideal chains on which to validate are those that have a low barrier for validation entry (i.e. permissionless networks) and no bonding requirement for validators (down the road this could change)
