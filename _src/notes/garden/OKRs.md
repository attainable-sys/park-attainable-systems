Objectives and Key Results. They must always be agreed upon and debated with those who are required to hold themselves by their directives, and because of this do take time to outline.

O/KRs are not a top-down process - they cannot be outlined by a manager so that team members fall in line with the goals. Instead, they have to be set collectively, through a process of listen -> clarify -> debate -> decide. Any OKRs that are set from the top will not represent the needs of the team.

	- Radical Candor - Kim Scott
	- [[Workflow]] - Informal Systems

- An OKR is something that is a description of an outcome, not an output --> KPIs measure the effectiveness of your outcomes, **not** your inputs.
