Last's week's discussion covered whether or not privacy is a failing movement, to which the answer is an urgent and resounding  'no'.

Privacy is a necessary part of resisting the ever-increasing attack surface of the internet. A private internet is a safe internet.

-

Community members chimed in to conclude that on an internet where more and more is visible, privacy is not about hiding things, it's about being in control of what people can see.

-

This means that privacy is not absolute, and this means you should get to decide what degree of information you want others to know about you, whether as a person or in your interactions with others.

this is what it means to have a 'culture of privacy'

-

the right kind of internet should make you feel safe - privacy is crucial to this, whether safe from harm or safe from observation.

h/t @vpr; the internet should feel 'cozy' - users should feel safe both on their own and in all the communities they are a part of.
