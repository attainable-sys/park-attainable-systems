I believe that leadership is how someone can move energy through a system and away from the places where it gets stuck. I've been a natural leader much of my life - I was always the kid that people picked to lead projects when someone needed to do it, and I've also always worked to never be the smartest person in the room because I'm always curious about how I can be in the service of the change that I want to see.

I'm now in a place in my career where I have significant leadership responsibility that is wholly undirected, and I want to take charge of this opportunity to both see out what I think success is in this situation and to hone in on the leadership qualities that I know I possess as a person so that they can be developed and despite any lack of support in doing so.

Further, I see a lot of opportunity in my current position because of the lack of accountability, and I want to optimize for my wants and desires in the opportunity while still urging the work toward the best possible version of success.

In all, I'm reaching out because I want to develop personal accountability, discipline, and the ability to celebrate my success as a leader, while also growing this skill to greater and more important heights in the future. I was apparently born to use these skills, so now I want to make the effort to use them.
