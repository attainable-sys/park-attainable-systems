A good meeting is run with an agenda:

- Q1 - What is the reason for the meeting?
- Q2 - Negotiate the outcome for the meeting
	- --> (Here's what we'll discuss)|(How much time it will take)|(outcome)
	- e.g. Discuss dept structure - 10 mins - outline roles and decide who owns what
- Q3 - Execute meeting
- Q4 - Did we meet results? Were we on time? What value was produced?
