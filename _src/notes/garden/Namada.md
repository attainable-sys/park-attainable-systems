Namada is two things: a fully-fledged [[protocol]] seeking to be adapted in order to ensure [[Privacy]] guarantees at the account level - but then, more crucially, Namada is a [[network]] as defined by the connections, individuals, the historical state of the protocol, and the movement of the Namada token across all connected domains of the network.

he [[Namada mission (notes)]] is a question of understanding how to adapt privacy across the entirety of the extant [[blockchain]] stack, due to a historical design trend of blockchains around transparency that results in compromise on the part of individual users.

On its technical face, Namada is an asset-agnostic privacy protocol that affords users [[account-level privacy]] guarantees through the use of a multi-asset privacy pool ([[MASP]]) that in turn allows for shielded actions from within the pool. At launch, Namada will ship as a complete protocol. This means that it will be feature-complete, and will not require additional development beyond the implementation that is intended. Namada intends to be a [[primitive]] that can be used across multiple domains in order to retrofit [[account-level privacy]] to anywhere that is compatible with an [[IBC]] connection.
