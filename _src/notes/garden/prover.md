a prover role has the responsibility to prove [[ZKP]]s into a merklized proof that can be posted to a contingent chain.

There is potential for a market for proving, similar to that which exists around transaction posting in the [[validator]] model for [[BFT]] [[proof-of-stake]] consensus mechanisms.
