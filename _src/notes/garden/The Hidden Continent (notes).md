## genre
speculative fiction - what if magic did exist, but it was not well understood and people will never understand it in the ways that people want to - because it's not science and can't be understood on those terms.
## setting
present-ish time - maybe some time in the 2020s? No reason not to write about it as though it's now.
## concept
There is an entire continent hidden between Australia, Asia, and South America - and there is magic there. The magic is wild and not something that people have control over, not to sufficient a degree that anything meaningful can be done with it, which is why people don't include it in modern tech.

Not to mention, technology actually accelerated our understanding of magic, but nothing was actually seen as useful from using the phenomenon in a meaningful way, so technology *is* what advanced society, not magic. Magic is powerful, but it's a fringe science.
