**1. Purpose**

- What problem does the idea serve?
	- I think that getting people to worship [[hope]] is what can happen in place of thinking about the [[loss of god]] and how aimless people have felt since there was this happening to humans. The problem is that people no longer have [[faith]] and I think this is something that has led to general apathy on the part of the public who could otherwise be putting themselves in a position to change things - we’ve allowed the way the world works to become entrenched, and I don’t think we need to put up with that.
- Who will use it?
	- The general public. I don’t want to call this religion, I want to call this an ideology. I don’t know how it is that you get people to move into a state of feeling as though they can change things, but I know that it’s something important I want to see people actually end up there because I think people are sort of unhealthy right now.
- How does it align with the world’s strategic goals and initiatives?
	- Well - the main thing is that I don’t think we’re really thinking about humanity’s goals and initiatives enough. I think we need to be looking at the critical perspective of understanding how to inspire people with health and a vivacity for living instead of lapsing into [[hopelessness]], which is the anti-theme that exists around [[hope]]. [[hopelessness]] has an [[antimemetics|antimemetic]] quality that is ultimately deleterious to the idea of creating networks, and I see that [[hope]] has a high memetic quality that I think can overcome this.
	- For humanity, I think being hopeful is constructive, it’s something that serves the outcome of thinking about humanity surviving beyond their current state, so knowing this I think is what is required in order for people to better survive the capitalist hellscape that is being created around the current way that humanity is working.
