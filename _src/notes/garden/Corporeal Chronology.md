Allows us to think about our ways of being by respecting a notion of chronotypical states, wherein we can acknowledge the biophysical aspects of how humans perceive and interact with [[state change]] that are constituted as time. Specifically, [[Rushkoff]] refers to this in [[Present Shock]] in relation to [[Filippi]]'s examination of moon-guided [[Corporeal Chronology]], acknowledging the following:‘

| Moon Phase | Neurotransmitter   | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| New        | [[Acetylcholine]]  | Focused, mentally engaged energy that peaks 7 days into the cycle - a good time for moving through states of efficiency that feel grounded in reality, for moving the body, or taking stock of one's surroundings and 'performing' - this is the best time of the month for making an effort. |
| Waxing     | [[Serotonin]]      | Composed, sedate, euphoria that can bring us further into the mind in order to engage with a sense of self - "We usually benefit from solitude at this time"                                                                                                                                  |
| Full       | [[Dopamine]]       | This is 'party mode', where we are highly motivated by the progression of reward, during this time 'reward-based learning' is well applied and can have positive effects on someone's ability to perform and think.<br>Set up rewards and [[incentive]]s during this time, since it's the best possible time for doing so and feeling the positive effects.                                                                           |
| Waning     | [[Norepenephrine]] | This is a period of 'reptilian' thought, mostly a state for performing mechanically and reaching 'flow states', since much of action will feel unconscious.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
Key to understanding all of this is that (per [[Filippi]]) there are 'daily cycles' that coordinate with these lunar cycles - the earlier in the cycle, the more effective someone will be at different times in the day.
