Flow bias is the quality of a [[system]]'s flow of energy as a resource between components. Sustainable [[flow bias]] is an equal exchange of energy (either [[short-chain]] or[[ long-chain]]) between components, while unsustainable [[flow bias]] results in an entropic flow of energy between components that eventually leads to less energy available for the system to function.
