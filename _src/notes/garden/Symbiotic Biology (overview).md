In the universe of [[The Cruft]] - the narrative of the story's universe has resulted in a [[speculative biology]] of the planet that had to evolve under the immense pressure of massive fungal expansion.
As a result of the [[Cruft event]], humanity wiped itself and most biology out. However, as a result of the Cruft event's massive flooding event across the planet led to perfect conditions for the evolution of fungi that were heavily resilient and well attuned to the conditions of a new earth. Whatever was left after massive de-and-rehydration did not have much latitude to continue on with earth dominance as biology, leaving room for the massive expansion of various and new fungal species that progressively colonized the planet.

This led to a period of the planet known as the [[Spore Earth]], where the fungal species that developed as a result of the [[Cruft event]] dominate the planet in its entirety. This period of earth was massively long - the conditions were harsh enough that the pace of evolution was allowed to proceed at the rate that one would expect. Over the course of billions of years, any remaining [[motile organisms]] on the planet developed various symbiotic and/or parasitic relationships with the fungi that have evolved across the planet, resulting in a biology that is a drastic departure from biology that is recognizably that of the earth before the [[Cruft event]].

Some notable organisms that evolved under these conditions are:
- [[Lilypad Hippo]]
- [[Walking Forest]]
- [[Fungus ants]]
