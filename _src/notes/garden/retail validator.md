A [[retail validator]] seeks to make their margins off of the percentage of commission that they earn from their stakers. As such, much of their success is contingent on how a community of tokenholders who are not at the institutional level and the reputation that they hold as an operator within the community of operators.
The means through which they can do this is through garnering popularity, participation in [[protocol governance]], and [[core contribution]] to [[protocol research]] - since the reputation and margin for the operation of a [[retail validator]] increases with the amount of stake delegated to their node.
