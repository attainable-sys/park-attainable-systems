## Principles
1. **Avoid all dogmatism** - do not believe in any particular gods or goddesses or any 'fundamental truths' and believe things that are a feature of psychology
	1. Access of the psychological archetypes of one's own mind - accessing mental states in the psych.
	2. Belief in belief itself - understanding that a practice can come from contemplation of different practices, and to move between them.
	3. No gods and goddesses are real
2. **Personal Experience** - nothing is true from other's experiences - learn from your own experiences, knowing that not knowing the answers and experimenting is the first step to personal understanding.
3. **Technical Excellence** - What are the results, what records can come from experimenting through different practices and what they mean to you.
4. **Deconditioning the mind** - Reducing and removing all the biases and are imposed upon a person, including the influence of the ego in order to open your eyes to the reality of what exists beyond the self.
5. **Diverse Approaches** - Adoption of any set of practices to seek out magic - A chaos magician is open to anything any everywhere.
6. **Gnosis** - Finding an altered state of consciousness in the course of practice, in order to remove biases.
