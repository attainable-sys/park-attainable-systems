Ideally, a 1:1 meeting is an environment within which a manager is there to help their direct reports grow as individuals in the roles that they hold. This means that over the course of the work that they are doing, they are able to share in their successes and divulge their blockers with a manager, so that they feel much more comfortable in answering / responding to issues that stop them from being able to work.

Effective 1:1 meetings are helpful because they become an environment for feedback and critique so that both of those in the meeting (the manager and the direct report) have the ability to grow from the experience. A strong and available 1:1 setting is an environment within which growth is highly possible and planned for.
