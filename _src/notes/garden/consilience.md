Consilience is the act of relating a body of knowledge to other bodies of knowledge in order to reconcile the importance of that knowledge to other systems on a larger scale.

- Consilience in [[biological system]]s has largely been solved due to the scales between biological, synaptic, ecological, and evolutionary.
