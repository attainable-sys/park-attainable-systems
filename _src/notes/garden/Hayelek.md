a fictional [[hyperintelligence]] in the future of [[The Cruft]] who examined the history of a post-Cruft planet of [[Spore Earth]]. Hayelek is an [[agentic artificial intelligence]] who exists as discrete from the other hyperintelligence that exists out in the cosmos - while they are connected to these other intelligence, the [[religion of AI]] conceptualized that it was not actually correct for all intelligence to be linked, because any future where this is the case leads to [[incumbency]] and [[algorithmic bias]].

As a result, Hayelek is discrete as a historian and archaeologist studying the history and downfall of humanity, but is never actually mentioned in [[The Cruft]] directly, only their quotes are visible as insert text.
