The intention of blockchains has never been to make a profit.

The use of markets to create incentive structures for operation is useful as a primary function (and often if can definitely generate revenue), but the greatest strength of blockchain technology will be found in its ability to coordinate mutually disinterested parties around coordinated resources.

With IBC channels, we see one of the first examples of [[Ostrom]]'s CPR ([[Common Pooled Resource]]), wilfully managed by a prosocial community of Cosmos Chains connected via IBC.

The true mission of decentralized networks will center around the successful management of necessary structures.
