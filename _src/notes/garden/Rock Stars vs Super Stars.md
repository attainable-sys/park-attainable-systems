Kim Scott argues that in most organizations, you have Rock Stars and Superstars, two working styles who are focused on different kinds of growth.

## A 'Rock star'
Someone who is interested in maintaining their current job and is content with the work that they are doing. Focusing on these people means that you can put them in a position to stabilize a team around the work that can be done because it becomes repeatable.

## A 'Superstar'

Needs constant growth in order to feel satiated by their work, and are looking to progress in both their career and prospects, so are always looking for other opportunities.

---
Useful directives here are:
1. Create a culture of guidance, both from leadership but also from within
2. Understand what motivates someone
3. Drive [[Collaborative Results]]

Responsibilities and relationships feed off of one another - you want to create a virtuous cycle between the two.
