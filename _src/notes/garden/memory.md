Memory is the shape of objects and experiences that exist in a space that is no longer there.

"Memory, you realized a long time ago, is a game that a healthy-brained person can play all the time, and the game of memory is won or lost by one criterion: Do you leave the formation of memories to happen-stance, or do you decide to remember?"
--> [[Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow]] - Gabrielle Zevin

Uban - the concept from the Penan that explores the idea of things that have left behind a space where they once were. This expands into the [[eschatology]] of Penan culture that causes their nomadism - due to never wanting to occupy the space of a ghost.
