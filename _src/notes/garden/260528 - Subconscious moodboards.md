	The pen I write with is a Schaeffer Prelude

---

How do you ask the universe for what you want?
That's not the right question, babe.
The right question is: what version of you wants what you're looking for?
years ago when I was switching careers, I read a bunch of books trying to help me figure it out. A friend of mine recommended "What color is your parachute"
at the time, it was the perfect exercise.
When opportunities came my way, I was sure of what I wanted to do because I'd done the work to figure it out - and I saw a lot of success, because I was fully invested in the work.
Here's the catch: Now that it's been a few years, I went so far down the track of the opportunities I thought I wanted at the time that by the end of it, I burned out, tired.
I didn't want to do anything anymore.
What was the reason, though?
It wasn't that the opportunities were bad.
It was that I'd fundamentally changed as a person.
A huge part of the reason I was burning out is because the job wasn't what I was looking for anymore.
This seems obvious, but the reality is that it's not.
Our needs change constantly.
It's exhausting to realize this, I know, but what it really means is that the version of you that you used to be
might still be the version of you guiding you toward what you think you want. It means you have to work inward.
this is called 'revisiting your priors' for the decisions you make - with the understanding that your values, demands, and desires, they're all very likely to change.
So what am I doing about it?
I'm re-reading the Parachute book because it worked for me, and it's helping me get clear on the fact that the present version of me is dramatically different from who I used to be.
It means that now, I'm in touch with the fact that the present version of me is the version I'm working for, and means that when something comes by
that I know I want to do,
I won't have a doubt.
So the next time you're wondering about how to get the world to give you what you want, ask yourself first: what version of you is it that wants that?
