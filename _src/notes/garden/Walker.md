## Who is the Walker?

The walker is a character in [[The Cruft]] that lands on [[Spore Earth]] and begins to explore, eventually meeting [[The Figure]] in some as-yet unknown location, possibly on the edge of a promontory.

## Why doesn't the Walker speak?

I think the main reason for this is that I want the relationship between the Walker and [[The Figure]] to be one of understanding, and I don't feel the need to have actual dialogue between them, instead it can be the Figure who says the most. This means that most of the way that the Walker speaks will be in the form of ideas, written in *italics*. [[The Mind (Cruft Character)|The Mind]] on the other hand will will always be represented in **bold text**, but the Walker's thoughts remain within its head.

I can't really come up with a good reason why it is that the Walker doesn't speak - maybe the faculties are conveniently removed from its ability, or because it's not supposed to - it's meant to be a servant drone of some sort, and so is not meant to have the ability to speak... but I'm really not sure why it doesn't speak. It could be the case that I simply don't explain this, and that's fine with me, if I really think about it.
