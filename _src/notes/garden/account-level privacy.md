In the context of [[blockchain accounts]], [[account-level privacy]] is a question of where it is that your assets are being held - meaning, what identifiable information does a particular account hold on a particular user. Ideally, account-level privacy should be a question of the information that is being revealed to those with whom you transact, or at the least is an account of what it is that an individual stores and have the ability to decide that which is available to those who use the information held by an individual account.
