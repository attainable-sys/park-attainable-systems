Turbulence is the quality of a [[system]] that is at the edge of a [[stable state]] that is in [[phase transition]],
technically understood as a [[phase state]]. It is marked by a specific increase in [[frothiness]] - wherein the [[system]] oscillates rapidly with characteristics between two otherwise stable forms of action.

Turbulence can also occur where two interacting states that are not made to interact by force.

This means that turbulence is always characterized by a high-energy state, regardless of the incompatibility of interacting materials or states.
