An [[activist validator]] operates at the scale of, usually, a single or only a few chains. They operate with the full intention to act committed to the success of the chain that they are a part of, and their main market are the community of tokenholders who hold a middling but self-custodied number of assets.
Paradoxically, [[activist validator]]s are often the most value-aligned with the success of a particular [[blockchain]] - they are normally highly active in [[protocol governance]], especially in systems that employ [[token-based governance]] under [[delegated proof-of-stake]].
