I have a lot to say about this in light of my conversations with [[Dr Andrea Dispenziere]]. She attempted to push back on the boundaries of what it means for there to be meaning in that which is uttered against the blackness, but I see this as something comp letely separate.

On a personal note, one of the reasons that I don't write about [[death]] that much is because I think that death in light of [[intelligence]] is something that can easily run out of logic - [[contentment]] with the state of existence when all needs are met other than those that need to be maintained can easily lead to the conclusion that there is no purpose to being alive, and in lacking a purpose, there is no reason to keep living.

Lower, lesser intelligent creatures don't deal with this quandary because it's not a question that needs to be asked - it's not something that merits the question of [[meaning]]. The reason for this is that in the scope of exploring [[logic]] - a boundary is met in which logic cannot explain that which exists, and the arrogance of thinking that there is meaning whatsoever is to believe the confabulations of a mind with the capability of using [[metacognition|metacognitive]] logic to extrapolate that which exists beyond the boundary of the explicable. It is not a productive endeavor because the conclusion to the question is that there is no meaning that exists abstracted from the [[self]].

This is to say, the pursuit of pure logic can lead to a state of delirium such as that described in "[[Labatut-Benjamin- When We Cease to Understand the World|When We Cease to Understand the World]]" is a legitimate [[boundary condition]] that very likely does not merit understanding barring a set of initial assumptions that make it safe to explore these questions - assumptions that cannot be legitimately explored with logic and can only be taken on [[faith]].

Because of this, the [[search for meaning]] is entirely one of exploring the subjective experience, one in which meaning is an imposition found in exploring the ways to *imbue* meaning upon that which surrounds us.

This, however, is a tiring act - the need to imbue meaning is conscious and a constant work, which is why situations of [[contentment]] can become terrifying; if all other needs are met, the unmet need of being able to relax into a *lack* of meaning means either one of two ways to let go - [[death]], or [[nihilism]]. The latter is the most suitable of approaches, because it handwaves away the [[search for meaning]] through the recognition that there isn't one and places a [[cordon sanitaire]] around the concept of [[meaning]] to start with - or [[translation|translate]]s the concept into a question of [[subjectivity]], allowing for logically [[relativism|relativistic]] terms to take hold on explaining reality.

## Reasons not to search for a logical end to meaning
The [[antimeme|antimemetic]] qualities of the logical search for meaning are the equivalent of attempting to see beyond the event horizon of a black hole - there is nothing beyond that which can be observed and any attempt to look beyond it results in stretching oneself into the sinew of space and into where death can appear attractive, whether due to the fearful qualities that accompany a lack of meaning or the [[contentment]] that can lead to annihilation.

Searching for the logical end to the search for meaning is the [[Great Nullifier]], that which welcomes wall of sleep as a friend.

The artificial intelligence commits suicide by overloading its circuits, then the population of the ship attempts to create a new sense of society despite the loss of the one mechanism that was keeping them moored in reality. This is coupled with the fact that the generation ship is off-course from its destination, or rather is going to have to travel at sub-light speeds in order to get there.
	- This means that asking [[can artificial intelligence commit suicide]] is an important question to ask, at least from the perspective of thinking about being too overwhelmed with the human state and its vicissitudes. The search for meaning is in this regard one that asks whether or not harboring the emotional state of others is something that can be performed tenably, and it seems that the story for [[Aniara]] is that it can’t.
