Protocol governance is the act of selecting a plan for the maintenance of [[open-source software]]. In the context of [[blockchain]], [[permissionless protocols]] must have a sense of [[decentralized governance]] that oversees both the direction and the maintenance of the protocol itself - lacking this, a protocol can easily be seen to be lacking in the stability necessary to seek credibility in its longevity.
