## The Book in 3 Sentences
Radical Candor seeks to teach readers about how you should approach the teams you manage - with the intention to change their lives for the better. By inspiring a culture of honesty and clarity in the critique that people give one another, a team becomes a mutually refining force that pushes people toward the outcomes that they want for themselves, while still in alignment with those that they work with and toward the goals they take part in setting. Finally, it lays out a strong framework for how it is that people should pursue improving the teams that they steward, with an unconscious tendency toward [[prosociality]] - the recognition that a few weak people on a team does not mean that the team is weak.
