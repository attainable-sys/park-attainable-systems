## AnyTrust
Intending to be a low-fee optimistic rollup environment well suited to high-tx implementations s/a rating systems, gaming, mostly so that people can be in control of the assets they earn and own on the platforms/protocols they support.

## Architectural considerations:
- Rollup separate from [[Arbitrum]] using a [[data availability]] committee with a 3 week fraud-proving period.
	- As with other chains, DA will lower cost to execution
	- Intention is to serve EVM DeFi applications, specifically gaming, with a potential major partnership with a major internet social media presence (Reddit)
- Economics are tbd, but running a committee node will offer you fee-share
- L2-L2 communication through std committee bridge
- Potential to bundle validation and DA nodes
