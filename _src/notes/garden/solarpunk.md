Solarpunk is a alternate utopian ideal in which humans live in balance with the natural world rather than in abstraction from it (as with the [[cyberpunk]] ideology).

While this practice has been seen a pure utopian ideal for the future of humanity, the aesthetic vision is salient as a meme for human progress that is alligned with our climate ideals, rather than in opposition to it (as with other, more dystopian ideals).

Interestingly, both Solarpunk and Cyberpunk futures feed well into human cultural progression toward [[Kardashev Scale]] Type 1 civilization status.
