One of the best ways that you can inspire someone to make a big change that will benefit others is by giving them the responsibility earlier than they expect to receive it.

A prime example is having a particularly bright person apply for a doctorate program directly instead of going through a masters program first.

Much of the time, people will rise to the challenges that they are given, and can present them with the option to overachieve their expectations.

	-  ["The high-return activity of raising others’ aspirations"](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2018/10/high-return-activity-raising-others-aspirations.html) - Tyler Cowen
