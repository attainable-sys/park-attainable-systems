A trade credit agreement between individuals is a mutual arrangement where two parties agree to exchange goods or services without immediate monetary transactions. Instead, they extend credit to each other, allowing one party to receive goods or services upfront while deferring payment to a later date, often with agreed-upon terms and conditions for repayment. This informal arrangement enables individuals to facilitate transactions without the immediate need for cash or external financing.
