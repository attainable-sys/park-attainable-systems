Incumbency acts a paradigmatic stopper for change in a [[system]] - things that don't want to change because they like the current paradigm or reject the evidence of the next one.

Examples of this are political leaders from previous [[paradigm]]s who have the systemic advantage of already being place in the face of re-election, or leaders of scientific [[institutions]] that refuse to allow a scientific body of knowledge to shift into its next [[phase state]].
