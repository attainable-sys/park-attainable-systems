An [[in-house validator]] is often a validator operation that has been subsumed by a parent organization, usually a [[cryptocurrency exchange]] that is seeking to support a token internally and hopes to provide a reward rate to their customers on this basis, as well as skimming off of this inflationary reward to prevent the dilution of their own holdings against the market.

In these situations, the holdings of an exchange are often delegated to a single or a small number of accounts and the accounts internal to the exchange are not represented on the network for which the exchange's validators provide their services. The margins are, in this way, extremely low.
