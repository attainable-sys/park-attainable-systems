2022The DispossessedUrsula LeGuin
We should come home to ourselves with empty hands and only the gifts of ourselves to offer. To truly be anarchist is to employ the an extreme joy in disrupting your notions of ownership and egoism. You as an individual have plenty, but you as a person don't hold the right to own it. Anarchical love is to be free of our holdings and to live our lives with permission. Anarchism allows arbitrary lines to be crossed in order to constantly seek the consent and respect of others, and living a life with anarchism at its root allows one to live a life free of limitations that take from individual freedom.
