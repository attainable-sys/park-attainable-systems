stable state

2222.079 · 2222 systems

A stable state is a phase state wherein a system is low-energy, such that there is decreased turbulence in the system.

A stable state is the general rule of most systems - stability is low-energy, therefore efficient, and it's the efficiency of energy use in a stable system that affords it a broad dominance against any variety of conditions, in stark contrast to the turbulence that exists at the edge of phase transitions.
