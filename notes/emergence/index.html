emergence

2222.028 · 2222 systems

Emergence is the unqualified threshold process by which phenomenae of high complexity come into being from a system of simple initial conditions.

What is an emergent quality?

How does emergence relate to thinking in systems and human society?

One major difference between the larger emergent system and the individuals that make up the system is an assumption that the larger system is more intelligent than the sum of its parts - and it's actually not. If anything, many emergent qualities of a complex adaptive system are motivated by relatively simple goals, meaning that the larger system is ticking toward a small set of metrics.

As mentioned in Economics for the future – Beyond the superorganism - ScienceDirect:

In principle a superorganism could be super intelligent but ours is not. In the 1930s economists chose GDP as a metric to track economic activity, not as an end goal.

The human species is not specifically intelligent as a whole, it is simply complex. The complexity of the overarching system does not map to intelligence.
