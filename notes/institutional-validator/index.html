institutional validator

2000.043 · 2000 blockchain

An institutional validator is often an operation whose profit margins depends on delegation by institutional-level holders of tokens in blockchain and cryptocurrency networks. The targets of this type of validator are individual wallet accounts, usually investors in protocols themselves, or foundations that are holding portions of the assets for a network.
As such, this kind of validator is primarily concerned with a small-but-mighty clientele that is interested in the success of the networks that they support, but as an infrastructure provider, do not feel any real obligation to the maintenance of the network from a protocol governance perspective, due to a lack of incentive to do so. Institutional validators can operate white-label validator nodes at 100% commission on-chain, then settle with the investor off-chain.
