system design

2222.082 · 2222 systems

The notion of system design comes from examining the interaction of all parts in a system and working to guarantee that there is a degree of sustainable interaction between the sum of parts. design as a notion in and of itself can be either emergent or intentional - an intentional design being one that was put into motion by an agent.

All system design can result in highly complex architecture, though to some degree intentional system design seems to leave out a lot of interaction with surrounding ecosystems, often leaving out sufficient examination of impact on surrounding ecosystem services 1. Unintentional system design, by contrast, tends to emerge from interaction with the environment on the part of an agent, such that a system will be designed in response to the pressures put upon the system. The latter tends to be much more sustainable and much more complex, since the system exists in concert with the environment and flow bias is at least to some degree in equilibrium between energy flowing from the environment into the system, then from the system back into the environment.
