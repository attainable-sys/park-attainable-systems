synthesis

1100.007 · 1100 intelligence and learning

The act of synthesis is the composition of information into the most important concepts for understanding.

A good understanding is as follows, using the fundamentally intelligible term 'heart-attack':
1. Prior the explicit knowledge of what happens in the body when the some one dies of a heart-attack, people simply died without anyone understanding why this was the case.
2. Some early physicians explored the problem, establishing a knowledge of the human body and recognizing what happens the heart fails.
3. This lead to deeper knowledge of things like the heart as a muscle, the nerves that lead signals to the heart, and what happens when these structures are interrupted
4. The physicians use this knowledge to interpret the event at hand, leading to descriptions such as
- myocardial infarction
- cardiac arrest
- etc.
5. This knowledge is then synthesized by the public to be understood as a fundamentally intelligible concept - the 'heart attack', wherein the event of someone dying from this condition is the result of the 'heart being attacked'.
6. As a result, the knowledge has been synthesized into the term 'heart-attack'
.
