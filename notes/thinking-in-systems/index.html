thinking in systems

2222.083 · 2222 systems

Thinking in systems means recognizing the connections between elements of different phenomena that work together in execution, such that the system design correlates closely with the model of interaction that different components of a system have in response to one another.

When we think in systems, we allow ourselves to explore the connections between different parts, as well as a higher level, non-granular differentiation between systems (see: Markov Blanket), allowing us to see how subsystems interact with the larger systems to which they are constituent, as well as affording us the chance to more easily assess where issues are arising.
