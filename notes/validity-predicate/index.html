validity predicate

2000.075 · 2000 blockchain

In relation to Anoma, a validity predicate is a way for a party to post a transaction to the network to be seen by both the intent gossip framework and the matchmaker nodes for the network.

Generally, a 'validity predicate' is a pre-ascribed tx intention - a desire for a certain outcome that be summarized and posted. A validity predicate is 'nullified' once the conditions for the validity predicate have been met and the transaction has been atomically settled with whatever intent matches the request.

Questions

-- Doesn't the concept of an intent predicate that in order for an intent to be settled, there must be an equal and opposite intent to meet it?
This strikes me as an issue, because once you request that an intent be settled, there must exist this 'opposing intent' unless there is a solving environment that can solve for a range of conditions and is willing to have them available provided an intent requesting settlement is posted and discovered by a solver.
