Magic as local logic

1050.021 · 1050 writing · 2026.01.21

How do you get the universe to believe something that it doesn't believe is true?

-- the universe of multiple people as single consciousness, above the clouds, with the polluted lands below
Magic that is a manipulation of physical logic and is a result magic. Logic is local and a result of communicating between the observer principle and the matter of an environment.
They lit the space, the light seeming to come from everything around them.
"How did you light this place? I see no light."
"I convinced this room that we could see everything inside of it."

Half of living in New York is having connections, but we should really get more specific about what we mean when we talk about connections. Connections are not necessarily the people with whom you have schmoozed at a party, but rather the edges of your network that do interesting things and show you the unexpected.
