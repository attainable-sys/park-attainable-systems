intent

2000.044 · 2000 blockchain

An intent is a posted desire of any responsive system - if I intend to do something, I am under the impression that it will be resolved by the conditions of my body's response to the request that I will do it. If I intend to purchase something, the conditions of the purchase are met provided what I offer in exchange (a barter condition) can be presented at the time that the thing I intend to purchase is offered to me.

In the context of Anoma, an intent is a partially expressed transaction that is settled once the conditions of the request are met; once the transaction has been settled, all intent note associated with the transaction are nullified and destroyed as a multi-party transaction takes place.

Issues:

Doesn't the concept of an intent predicate that in order for an intent to be settled, there must be an equal and opposite intent to meet it?
This strikes me as an issue, because once you request that an intent be settled, there must exist this 'opposing intent' unless there is a solving environment that can solve for a range of conditions and is willing to have them available provided an intent requesting settlement is posted and discovered by a solver.
