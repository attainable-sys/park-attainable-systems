Warmup routine

1017.003 · 1017 running · 2026.01.24
  1. Warm-up (8–10 min)
    1. 3–5 min easy row / jog / brisk walk• 2×10 bodyweight good mornings
    2. • 2×10 glute bridges
    3. • 1×10 scap pushups
    4. • 1×10 band pull-aparts
  2. Barbell Block (posterior chain + legs, ~20 min)
    1. A1. Barbell RDL – 4×6–8 (RPE ~7, 75–90s rest) - 100lbs
    2. A2. Front squat or high-bar back squat – 3×5–6 (leave 2 reps in reserve, 75–90s rest) - 120lbs
  3. Landmine + Climbing Strength (~15–18 min)3 rounds, ~60s rest between rounds:
    1. Landmine deadlift or landmine RDL – 8 reps - 22lbs
    2. • Landmine row – 10/side
    3. • Then 2–3 scap pulls – 44lbs
    4. • Offset dead hang (band/jug assist as needed) – 10–20s/side
  4. Cable / Pulldown Climbing Block (~12–15 min)
    1. Single-arm cable row – 3×10–12/side
    2. • Straight-arm pulldown or cable pullover – 3×12–15
    3. • Cable face pull – 3×12–15• Short rests (30–45s) here to keep HR and sweat up
