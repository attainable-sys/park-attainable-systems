Crypto 5-year prediction

2000.056 · 2000 blockchain · 2022.07.08

This question is likely to be the very question funding all manner of speculative money and effort - any amount of research and investment into this is a set of bets placed on potential outcomes, all with the understanding that there is tremendous upside in placing the right set of bets. Is crypto an alternative to our current centralized banking system? Possibly, but this feels incomplete. Is it a new internet, with ownership at it's core? Again, possibly - but we'll have to see.

Truthfully, we don't know; no one does. Anyone who claims to know for sure is fooling others (and likely themselves) using the same skill of prognostication found in county-fair soothsayers - making predictions isn't something you can do with any level of certainty.

If we attempt to look into the future of the next five years, or even beyond, the data points we can use are scarce, but not invisible - like a pheromone trail for worker ants to follow through the jungle floor. And, just like ants, we can continue to find the morsels we bring back from these path to build a space we don't have a complete knowledge of understanding: no one ant knows how to build a colony. In the same way, no one human or company knows where crypto will end up.

Those who hold the widest and deepest kind of knowledge will win this game; being aware of the options that both exist and hope to exist well ahead of their attempt to come to life confers on the winner this enormous sense of upside, provided the information they used to set their prediction is the correct kind. It guarantees that the winner is there to see this future play out. We can prepare ourselves for potential future and by educating ourselves sufficiently on each of the paths that lead to them, so that we are prepared to move in that direction when the time comes, and support others when it does.

At Figment, we work toward finding the pieces of whatever it is that crypto will become. We work to build relationships with teams who hold the conviction that they are building toward an adjacent possible set of futures that lead from our current time. Can we act as though we know for certain what crypto will become? It's doubtful - but can we work to ensure that we are present to help build this future? Certainly.

We work with teams like those at Solana, Avalanche, and Sui to build fast-moving, low-latency environments for asset ownership and transfer, because it seems that the future toward which we are headed needs these these environments. We work with teams like those behind Anoma, Penumbra, or Secret, who work to build liberating primitives because it seems that in this future, there is a need for liberating primitives.

We work with teams who are building new tools that will land in the hands of the next generation of developers, who will in turn use these tools to build components of something we can't see yet. We work with with these teams to secure the technology they are building under the impression that the pieces that are being built have the quality of the same pieces that will fit into a broader picture of what crypto will become.

All we do know for certain is that there is something here to support and help build, because the communities of developers who are working toward this joint vision have the same incomplete picture of something world-changing, and are working toward it tirelessly, regardless of the screaming wilds that surround them. As a result, we also work tirelessly to support it.

The market is a wilderness, we do not know what will be made within it. By working with Figment, you are working with a team of people who understand that while we can't predict the future for crypto, but who know for certain that we will have a hand in building it.

Come with us, and let's see what new tools we build within this wilderness.
