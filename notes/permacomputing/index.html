permacomputing

2222.060 · 2222 systems

Permacomputing is the approach of supporting a computational advent with the intentions of sustainability in mind - to keep things in context and to consider the environment within which it exists.

  • Care for chips
  • Care for life
  • Keep is small
  • Keep it flexible
  • Hope for the best and prepare for the worst
  • build on solid ground
  • Amplify awareness
  • expose everything
  • respond to changes
  • everything has a place
- [permacomputing.net](https://permacomputing.net/Principles/)
