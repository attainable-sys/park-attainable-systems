small world (information theory)

2222.074 · 2222 systems

Small world architecture is an ordered system with a small level of randomness and enough clustering so that the random connections act as highways for infromation transfer between nodes. Interestingly, under this kind of architecture, the first few connections matter the most in having an impact on network growth.

Small world networks have short chains (short path length), and high clustering around a number of well-connected nodes, which are considered the small-but-mighty connectors.
