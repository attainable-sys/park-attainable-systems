1017.006 · 1017 running · 2026.05.03

I need to think through what it is about the nest few weeks, into the next few months. What I need to build is a spreadsheet that I can manage a training schedule. It's time. I want to find a 50-miler trail run that I want to engage with that I'll have to train for. I'm at a decent baseline maintenance fitness right now, which is great, but I want to see what I can do about building out a fitness that goes beyond this - I want to get through the 50-miler without an injury, and then I need to set up for a trail relay race in mid october.

There are essentially four things that I need to work on:
1. a week-by-week training plan starting at a 25-mile baseline leading toward
- trail 50-miler in September
- a high elevation relay race in mid-october, wherefrom I'll head to Hong Kong

This plan will need to account for the fact that:
- I'm climbing 2/3 days a week
- Mondays are quality work
- Thursdays are going to mostly be climbing endurance work
- I'm looking to lift three days a week (arms / legs / core)
- Tuesdays should be a speedwork day for running
- I need to eventually ramp up to whatever mileage makes the most sense for the endeavors

My plan is to get really jacked. We've already established that I have a pretty great level of fitness, but I'm definitely out of it right now. I have a schedule where I wake up around 6am to start writing (I am starting this tomorrow, but have been trending in that direction lately), and I seek to start any exercise around 11am-12pm each day, depending on how much writing I've done in the morning. What I want the most from this plan is that we're cross-referencing the best training plans that exist out there against what I'm capable of based on my metrics.

It should show each day across the top of the sheet's columns and each week along the plan's rows I'm going to add a spreadsheet that I used last training season that you can use for reference and to build off of, and I want to account for the fact that what I have the hardest time with is nutrition and understanding my actual caloric needs in order to get the fitness I'm seeking out.

Suggest where the plan can be improved upon. Be ambitious, I can keep up. Importantly, you know that I'm working alongside this on my work and will start working beyond this on other things. This fitness schedule is my anchor, the work happens around it.
